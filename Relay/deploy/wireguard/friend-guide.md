# Playing OpenCiv3 online with me

You install one small app once. It makes a private link to my computer that carries only our game. Your normal internet and files are not touched. About 5 minutes.

I will send you a small file called `<your name>.conf`. Treat it like a key: don't share it.

1. **Install WireGuard.** Go to **wireguard.com/install** and download it for your computer (Windows or Mac) and install it. On a phone, install "WireGuard" from the app store, but play on a computer.
2. **Load my file.** Open WireGuard. Click **Import tunnel(s) from file** (or drag my `.conf` file onto the window). Pick the file I sent you.
3. **Switch it on.** Click **Activate**. It should say **Active**. Turn it on when we play and off afterwards.
4. **Join.** Start OpenCiv3, choose **Join online**, and paste the invite I send. It looks like `KQ7-4MZ@ws://10.8.0.1:8080` — paste all of it, including the part after the `@`. You don't need to change any setting.

**If it doesn't work:** check WireGuard says **Active**; check we're on the same game version; tell me what the game says. If it says Active but nothing joins, tell me, it may be on my side.
