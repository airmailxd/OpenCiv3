# 4. Science, techs and wonders

This section covers research cost, beakers, eras, tech trading, the AI's research choices, free-tech sources, and all Great and Small Wonders in Conquests (C3C 1.22, with Vanilla 1.29f / PTW 1.27f differences where sources give them). Scenarios, mods and the special C3C game modes are out of scope.

Source key. Local files read for this section, from the Steam "Civilization III Complete" install (`.../Sid Meier's Civilization III Complete/`):
- **BIQ** = `Conquests/conquests.biq` (C3C 1.22 default rules), read with this repo's `QueryCiv3` (BLDG, TECH, RULE, WSIZ, DIFF sections). Building costs are stored at 1/10 scale ("In Civ3 files, building costs are stored at 1/10th of their actual value", `C7Engine/C7GameData/ImportCiv3.cs`). Shield costs below are BIQ x 10.
- **Pedia** = `Conquests/Text/Civilopedia.txt` (Firaxis' C3C Civilopedia). This is not OpenCiv3's `C7/Civilopedia/Text/Civilopedia.txt`, which is our own text.
- **README-V** = `README.txt` (Vanilla patch notes up to 1.29f). **README-PTW** = `civ3PTW/readme.txt`. **README-C3C** = `Conquests/readme.txt`. **EdSupp** = `Conquests/EditorSupplement.txt`.
- **EDHELP** = the official C3C editor help, `Conquests/Civ3ConquestsEdit.chm` (decompiled), cited by page name.
- **MANUAL** = the Steam manual PDF, https://cdn.akamai.steamstatic.com/steam/apps/3910/manuals/manual.pdf (text extract; page numbers are the printed folios and approximate). It is mostly base-game text (see the note in section 2).

## 4.1 Research cost

- **Formula (Vanilla 1.29f, reverse-engineered).** Source: https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/research-cost-formula-v1-29f.29485/ — "Research Cost = [MM * [10*COST * (1 - N/[CL*1.75])]/(CF * 10)] - Research done so far". The thread also gives a simplified form: "Research cost = DOWN{MM*DM*(COST/100) *[1 - (N/(CL*1.75))]} - Research done so far." The CivFanatics FAQ repeats the first form: https://civfanatics.com/civ3/faq/ — "Research Cost = [MM * [10*COST * (1 – N/[CL*1.75])]/(CF * 10)] – Research done so far".
  - **MM** (map size factor) — "MM = map modifier(tech rate on world sizes tab in the editor)". BIQ WSIZ tech rate: Tiny 160, Small 200, Standard 240, Large 320, Huge 400. These match the thread's values.
  - **COST** — "COST = technology cost as on the civilization advances tab in the editor." The BIQ cost is the base (for example Bronze Working 3, Integrated Defense 360).
  - **Check on a Standard map.** https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/what-will-the-ai-research-next.45559/ — "For a standard map (tech rate = 240), the AI needs 24 times the base cost of each technology in beakers." alexman's example: "So if Bronze Working were an empty tech, it would have a value of 256/18=14", where 18 = 72 beakers / 4 per turn.
  - **N, CL** ("civs that know it" discount) — "N = number of civs on the diplomacy screen that have discovered the tech." "CL = number of civs left in the game". Only contacted civs count toward N. Version note from the same thread: "CL*1.5 in 1.21f is now CL*1.75 in 1.29f." The 1.21f thread gives a different rounding form: https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/ai-trading-valuation.21925/page-2 — "Research cost = MM*COST/10 - UP{MM/100 * UP[COST*10N/DOWN(CL*1.5)]} - Research done so far".
  - **CF / DM** (difficulty) — "CF = AI cost factor(as on the difficulty tab in the editor)" and "For the purposes of the research cost formula, CF has a maximum value of 10." Qitai's correction in the same thread: "Cost factor = {20, 12, 10, 9, 8, 6} for the various difficulty level", which gives DM {10, 10, 10, 11.1, 12.5, 16.7}. The author confirms: "the Monarch figure is 11.1". BIQ DIFF cost factors in C3C: Chieftain 20, Warlord 12, Regent 10, Monarch 9, Emperor 8, Demigod 7, Deity 6, Sid 4. Implementation reading: research cost x 10/min(CF,10), so x1.0 at Regent and below, x1.11 Monarch, x1.25 Emperor, x1.43 Demigod, x1.67 Deity, x2.5 Sid. The Demigod and Sid values are an UNVERIFIED extrapolation, because the thread predates PTW's extra levels.
  - **Whom it applies to.** Same thread: "This formula is relevant for research cost of the AI civs, not just the human player" and "techs are now more expensive for the human player on Monarch, Emperor and Deity." See Disputed, item 1.
  - **Since 1.29f the AI pays the same (Regent) price at every level; the difficulty factor scales the human's cost.** alexman (by then a Firaxis software engineer), https://codehappy.net/apolyton/threads/66516-1.htm (Apolyton "The Chieftain Research Myth", 4 Nov 2002) — "On Chieftain, technologies used to cost the same as Regent for the human, but were twice as expensive for the AI." / "But since 1.29f, Chieftain technologies cost half as much for the player, and are constant for all levels for the AI". alexman again, https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/what-will-the-ai-research-next.45559/page-2 (#31, 30 Jun 2003) — "the research cost of each tech for the AI is the same for all difficulty levels." / "The AI gets a production bonus (or penalty) which exactly cancels the increase (or decrease) in cost". Qitai (#32) dates it: "the research cost changes is effective vanilla civ3 1.29, not PTW." The cost-factor editor text agrees that CF is about the AI: EDHELP "Cost Factor (AI Bonuses)" — "Determines the cost factor that is applied to AI growth, shields, and research. The player's cost factor is always 10." C3C confirmation (player): https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/late-game-tech-rate-confusion.696592/ ([C3C], tjs282, 2025) — "the AI always pays the 'Regent' price for an unknown tech" and "your 'unknown tech' beaker-costs will be 1/0.8 = 1.25x times the AI's" at Emperor. C3C multiplayer check: https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/technology-list-and-cost.193586/ (AutomatedTeller, C3C MTDG, 2007, Emperor, Standard map) — "the cost of pottery is 2, which if you use that formula, becomes 60 (240 *10*2 / 8)." Owner ruling for the engine: the human factor is capped at 10 (no discount below Regent). alexman's "half as much for the player" on Chieftain conflicts with that cap; see Disputed, item 1.
  - **Exact rounding.** TechCalc 2.01 (a tool "For all versions of Civilization III. (Patched versions)"), https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/utility-techcalc.38631/ (Grey Fox, Dec 2002) — "ResearchCost = Down((MM/CF)*(Cost-Up.1(Cost*N/Down(CL*1.75))))"; mydisease's restatement in the same thread: "ROUNDDOWN((MM/CF) * (Cost - (.1 * ROUNDUP(10 * COST * N/ROUNDDOWN(CL * 1.75)))))". Here MM/CF is the per-civ multiplier (24 at Regent on a Standard map), and CL counts the civs left, including your own. With 5 civs, each known holder cuts 1/8.75 of the cost: fe3333au, same 193586 thread — "The cost reduces by 11.43% of the Beaker Cost each time another known civ pocesses it."
- **Future techs.** BIQ RULE: Future Tech Cost = 400. The editor field was added in Vanilla (README-V: "Editor: On General Settings, added Future Tech Cost."). EDHELP "Future Tech Cost" — "Determines the cost of each "future technology". Default is 400." MANUAL p.125: after the last named advance "they can begin researching futuristic advances" / "When your civilization accumulates enough scientific research to finish one unit of Future Technology, you can research another. Each Future Technology you discover adds to your final score". "Each" in EDHELP suggests a flat base cost (400 in place of the BIQ tech cost, then the usual multipliers). How 400 enters the formula, and whether later future techs cost more, is UNVERIFIED.
- **Integer rounding.** Commerce-to-beaker conversion floors, so some beakers are lost. https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/the-research-slider.662300 (vorlon_mi) — "Some of those calculations use the "FLOOR" function, to produce an integer result that drops the fractional parts."

## 4.2 Minimum and maximum research time

- **C3C: 4 to 50 turns.** BIQ RULE: MinimumResearchTime = 4, MaximumResearchTime = 50. https://civfanatics.com/civ3/faq/ — "For all three games, the minimum research length is 4 turns." and "For **Conquests**, that was upped to 50 turns as the maximum."
- **Vanilla history.** README-V (1.16f): "The maximum number of turns it can take to research an advance has been increased from 32 to 40." README-V (1.29f): "Editor: On General Settings, added Min. Tech Turns." / "added Max. Tech Turns."
- **What the maximum means.** https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/the-research-slider.662300 (justanick) — "The time for researching a tech is limited to 4 turn as the minimum and 50 turns as the maximum." and "You cannot use up more than 50 turns of actual research, no matter how little you spend". jarred! in the same thread: "it'll still take the 50 turn max to finish a 200 beaker tech". Reading: as long as at least some research is funded, the tech completes on turn 50 at the latest.
  - **One beaker a turn is enough (1.29f/PTW).** https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/the-role-of-the-specialist-citizen.108789/ (Bede, Jan 2005, on 1.29f and PTW scientists at 1 beaker) — "If you employ only one you can research any technology at a total cost of 40 beakers at a rate of one beaker per turn." That is the 40-turn cap of those versions. C3C: alexman (Firaxis Games Software Engineer), https://apolyton.net/forum/miscellaneous/archives/civ3-strategy-archive/117372-50-turn-techs (4 Nov 2003) — "scientists now give 3 beakers each, so you actually waste research potential when researching a tech with a single scientist and 0% science." The waste only makes sense if the tech arrives at the cap however little is spent. So the cap is a forced completion after the maximum number of funded turns, not a cost reduction. Whether a turn with zero beakers counts toward the 50 is UNVERIFIED.
- **Editor text.** EDHELP "Maximum Research Time" — "Determines the maximum number of turns in which any technology can be researched. Default is 40." (the help text predates the C3C BIQ value of 50). EDHELP "Minimum Research Time" — "Determines the minimum number of turns in which any technology can be researched. Default is 4."
- **What the minimum means.** https://apolyton.net/forum/miscellaneous/archives/civ3-strategy-archive/42626-distilled-civ3-tips-and-notes?p=953406 (David Weldon) — "Yes, there is a minimum of 4 turns for a tech advance, no matter how much overkill you use." See Disputed, item 2. Whether beakers above the 4-turn pace are lost or banked is UNVERIFIED.
  - **Tested floor, and where 3-turn techs come from (Vanilla 1.0x).** https://codehappy.net/apolyton/threads/33948-2.htm ("How science works and why there IS a 4 turn cap") — korn469 set the editor tech rate to 1 and "there was still a four turn rate cap"; David Weldon called that "the final confirmation". The 3-turn reports in the same thread come from changes during the research: Capt Dizle, "You can get tech in three turns if you start at four and your tech production increases during the 4 turn period", and "Probably has to do with the tech being discovered by an AI civ during the period I am researching it"; TrainWreck20 got two 3-turn techs "during my 'golden age'". Reading: the floor is applied when the cost is set, and a later rise in output or fall in cost can still finish the tech in 3 turns. This is UNVERIFIED for C3C.
- **Slider floor.** https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/the-research-slider.662300 (vmxa) — "at the start you cannot drop below 20% and research, unless you have a scientist running." Treat this as UNVERIFIED as a rule. It probably reflects the 50-turn cap, since research has to be fast enough to beat 50 turns.

## 4.3 Beaker overflow and loss

- **Excess from the completing city is lost; later cities feed the next tech.** https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/a-few-questions.225457 (TimTheEnchanter) — "The cities are processed one at a time in reverse order of their creation." and "Any excess beakers from the city that gets you to the next tech are lost." ElephantU, same thread: cities processed after the completing one add their research to the new tech.
- **Simpler player statements (all extra lost).** https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/are-extra-science-beakers-wasted.63391 (Rik Meleet) — "When a tech is researched all extra beakers are lost." https://civfanatics.com/civ3/faq/ — "there is no shield or food carryover for cities, just like there is no beaker carryover when researching a technology." See Disputed, item 3.
- **Measured loss.** The overflow mod's tracker found about 5% of beakers lost: https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/mod-tech-overflow.398296/latest — "generated 7,562 research and lost 375 (4.96%)" (as reported in search results; not re-fetched).
- **A 2002 test of the overflow.** https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/science-slider-when-1-turn-from-tech-change-it-or-not.24350/page-2 (Jim, Vanilla, Jun 2002) — "The ~494 beakers were lost forever." / "Thus, excess beakers are lost." T-hawk (page 1): "I've tested this. The beakers don't carry over."
- **Switching research throws away the beakers (Vanilla manual).** MANUAL p.123 — "you can interrupt research in progress." / "Of course, by changing their focus, you lose all of their work on the advance you order them to abandon." Players at the time agreed: same thread page 1, Sir Yelof, "you lose all the beakers you put into researching a tech if you switch techs in midstream"; Tassadar, "you restart at 0"; regoarrarr, https://codehappy.net/apolyton/threads/33948-1.htm — "if you switch techs that research is gone forever." kring (24350 page 2): "Switch to another tech in the middle of a turn; you can then switch back." So a switch that is undone within the same turn costs nothing. T-hawk claimed that only half is lost (see Disputed, item 9). No later patch note changes this rule.
- **Receiving the tech you are researching.** MANUAL p.123 — "If by chance you're given the civilization advance your scientists are currently researching, your Science Advisor immediately switches the research effort to a new topic of your choice." README-V (1.16f): "Traded advances that cause an era transition, now allows the player to select the advance to research in the new era." Whether the stored beakers survive is disputed (Disputed, item 9).
- **Theory of Evolution discards stored beakers.** https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/theory-of-evolution-toe.60765 (Gengis Khan) — "All beakers stored when the ToE is completed are lost."
- **Research is applied at turn start.** Pedia GCON_Research — "At the start of each turn, the science output of each city is added to the research project currently in progress".
- **C3C scientists.** https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/civ3-conquests-additions-changes-list.104294/ (Arathorn) — "Scientists now produce 3 beakers per turn instead of 1."

## 4.4 Eras: required vs optional techs

- **Four eras.** BIQ ERAS: Ancient Times, Middle Ages, Industrial Ages, Modern Times.
- **Every required tech of the current era must be known before advancing.** https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/revolutionizing-the-tech-tree-mechanics.102803 (Plotinus) — "a civ cannot progress from one era to another without first researching all the non-essential techs from the earlier one". "Non-essential" is evidently a slip for "required". https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/certain-clarifications.22543 (Mr Spice) — "Yes, you have to possess them, but there are plenty of other ways to accuire techs than to research them on your own."
- **You cannot research into the next era early.** The structural evidence is in the BIQ. The first-tier techs of each later era have no prerequisites in the BIQ: Monotheism, Feudalism and Engineering (Middle); Nationalism, Steam Power and Medicine (Industrial); Rocketry, Fission, Computers and Ecology (Modern). The editor forbids cross-era prerequisites. https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/revolutionizing-the-tech-tree-mechanics.102803 — GRM7584: "Era-specific techs can only have prereqs in their era, or NONE-era"; Paasky quotes the editor error "The required tech must be in the same era". So the era gate, not prerequisites, is what keeps next-era techs closed. The manual says so directly: MANUAL p.123 — "To move forward from one scientific age to the next (and gain access to the advances in that age), you're required to successfully research almost all of the advances available in your current age. The only exceptions are advances that fall into the category of optional." Its list of optional Ancient advances (Horseback Riding, Literature, Monarchy, Republic) includes Horseback Riding, which the C3C BIQ below does not mark optional. The manual's list is older text; the BIQ is authoritative.
- **Trading and the Great Library cross eras.** https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/tech-prerequisites-in-previous-eras.472427/ (Quintillus) — "tech trading and the Great Library techs are always across eras." README-V (1.16f): "Traded advances that cause an era transition, now allows the player to select the advance to research in the new era."
- **Optional techs never block advancement.** BIQ flag `NotRequiredForEraAdvancement`. The editor checkbox is "Not Needed for Era Advancement", and the F6 chart marks these techs: https://apolyton.net/forum/miscellaneous/archives/civ3-general-archive/73736-what-technologies-don-t-you-need (vondrack) — "the optional techs have a slashed circle in the upper right corner."
- **Free techs above your era wait for their prerequisites.** EDHELP "Free Techs (Civilization Properties)" — "Note that advanced technologies may be given as "free techs" but a civilization will not receive them until all required prerequisites have been fulfilled." EDHELP "Era (Civilization Advance)" — "Selecting "none" will make the technology unavailable for research by any of the civilizations in the game, but you can assign that technology to any civilization as a "free tech"."
- **Era-None techs.** README-V: "Editor: Updated ERA_NONE techs to work as expected (you can never research them and they don't impede era advancement but they can be assigned as free techs)." README-PTW: if a civ cannot reach a non-era prerequisite, the techs sprouting from it "will not be required for advancement to the next era."
- **C3C optional techs (BIQ).**

| Era | Techs | Optional (not required) |
|---|---|---|
| Ancient | 21 | Literature, The Republic, Monarchy |

## 4.5 Conquests tech-tree differences (vs Vanilla/PTW)

- **Radio removed; Ironclads and Fascism added.** README-C3C (1.20): "Radio Tech removed", "Electronics requirement removed from Motorized Transportation", "Civil Defense requires Electronics", "Advanced Flight now requires Electronics", "Radar Towers require Advanced Flight". https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/civ3-conquests-additions-changes-list.104294/ (Arathorn) — Ironclads: "requires Steam Power, required for nothing, allows Ironclads unit, optional tech"; Fascism: "requires Nationalism, required for nothing, allows Fascism Government, optional tech". The BIQ confirms both (Industrial, optional, costs 100 and 130).
- **Philosophy became a flagged bonus tech.** Same thread: "Philosophy now gives a bonus tech to the first civ to research it." BIQ: Philosophy has `BonusTechToFirstCivThatResearches`.
- **Diplomatic tech moves.** Same thread (Arathorn): "Map trading moved from available with Map Making to Printing Press." and "Communications trading moved from available with Map Making to Navigation." README-C3C notes MPTournament rules moved map trading back ("MPTournament: Map Trading moved back to Map Making").
- **Tech count.** Thunderfall, same thread: "it is now 81 with the removal of Radio". The C3C BIQ has 83 era techs (21+22+23+17). The 81 figure is UNVERIFIED and may be pre-1.22 or may exclude the two new optional techs.
- **Tech purchase price.** Same thread (Arathorn): "Changed calculation on tech prices, in terms of gold required to buy." The new formula is UNVERIFIED.

## 4.6 Tech trading and tech brokering

- **Who can trade a tech.** https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/revolutionizing-the-tech-tree-mechanics.102803 (Hugin) — general techs "can be traded with any other civilization, as long as that civ has the prerequisite technologies". The BIQ has a `CannotBeTraded` tech flag; no C3C default tech sets it.
- **AI-to-AI trade rate.** BIQ DIFF "AI to AI trade rate", exposed in Vanilla (README-V 1.21f: "Editor: Exposed AI to AI trade rate."). C3C values: Chieftain 110, Warlord 120, Regent 130, Monarch 140, Emperor 150, Demigod 160, Deity 170, Sid 200.
  - **Meaning (Firaxis editor help).** EDHELP "AI to AI trade rate (AI Bonuses)" — "This value is a percentage (acceptable values range from 100% to 1000%) which is multiplied by the total value (in gold) of the initiating civ's offerings, which is used by the "other" AI civ to determine whether a deal is acceptable or not. For example, with a rate of 120, an AI civ offering 100 gold to another AI civ for something would actually be worth 120 gold to the other AI civ." / "This rate directly affects the frequency and aggressiveness with which AI civs trade amongst themselves." It does not apply to the human: WarpStorm, https://codehappy.net/apolyton/threads/68856-1.htm (Nov 2002) — "AI to AI trade rate only applies to AI players, not to the human players."
  - **Possibly only when the buyer is short (secondhand Soren Johnson).** Same thread, WarpStorm: "Soren said that the AI to AI trade rate is deficit spending only." Catt's reading: "only if the buying AI cannot afford full price will the Trade Rate come into play". This matches Soren Johnson (Firaxis), https://codehappy.net/apolyton/threads/42905-1.htm (20 Feb 2002, 1.17f era) — AIs "might be willing to sell a tech for down to half-value, but only if the other AI has nothing else to give for it." The EDHELP text describes no such condition. Whether the multiplier applies to every AI-AI deal or only to deals the buyer cannot otherwise afford is UNVERIFIED.
  - **Other Firaxis points on AI-AI trading (Vanilla 1.17f).** Same 42905 thread, Soren Johnson: "The AI's do not give techs away for free." AIs are "now doing per-turn gold offers for techs", and "They aren't allowed to sign per-turn deals they can't pay."
- **AI price drops as more civs know a tech; monopolies are dear.** https://civfanatics.com/civ3/faq/ — "techs are very expensive to buy or research when no one knows them". The FAQ also names the classic brokering trade: "called a '2fer' or 'xfer' (2 for 1)."
- **Brokering.** Buy a tech from one AI and resell it to the others. https://civfanatics.com/?p=171 ("Zero Percent Science") — the author calls himself a "Day Trader" and sells a tech for "a few gold per turn for 20 turns". https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/science-broker-analysis.10012 (Baron23) — "The AI also plays tech broker at times".
- **AIs pass techs on among themselves.** https://civfanatics.com/?p=170 — "they'll trade it amongst themselves if you give it to one so give it to all of them at the same time."
- **The AI's trade value differs from its research value.** https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/what-will-the-ai-research-next.45559/page-3 (alexman) — "The AI places a value on each tech for trading purposes, which (according to Soren) is actually different". Vanilla also changed this: README-V (1.21f) "Adjusted advance trading rates for AI." The full trade-valuation formula is UNVERIFIED and belongs to the diplomacy section.
- **Player-observed trade-value factors (C3C, no formula).** https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/how-do-ais-value-techs-in-market.126379/ (2005) — TimBentley: "The things that make the AI value techs more include enables government, drafting, or mobilization", "the more people that know a tech (particularly if it's a monopoly) will affect the price.", "Optional techs are worth less." Khan_Asparuh: "Is it optionnal - 2/3 of the price." and "If you already have some beakers invested in a tech, it's cheaper." TimBentley: "As far as I know a formula to determine the trade value has not been computed." A built wonder lowers the trade value of its tech but not its research cost: https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/how-much-techs-cost.62580/ (Catt, Aug 2003) — "the trade cost drops to 10 or 11 gold after the build" (Free Artistry after Shakespeare's) and "the research cost of Free Artistry stays unchanged by the building of Shakespeare's Theater". These are observations, not a tested formula.

## 4.7 AI research choice (alexman) — already in `C7Engine/AI/DevelopmentAI.cs`

- **Method.** https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/what-will-the-ai-research-next.45559/ — "The AI places a value on each technology it can research next. The values depend on the various things allowed by each technology, as well as on the turns needed by that AI civ". It was found "by stripping all technologies from their benefits, adding them back one by one". "These values are cumulative." The values feed a weighted random choice: "techs with higher value were chosen more often" (alexman, quoted in https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/manipulating-ai-tech-research.657856/), and tjs282 there: "still basically just a weighting-factor for a pRNG dice-roll".
- **Values.** "/turns" means divided by the turns the AI needs for the tech. Per turn: Empty tech 256, SS component 261, Government 259, Conscription 259, Mobilization 259, No trade 149. Flat: Defender (no resource) 198, Attacker (no resource) 134, Defender (resource required) 70, Naval transport 34, Resource 16, Double worker speed 8, Diplomats 8, MPP 8, ROP 8, Alliances 8, Embargoes 8, Trade over ocean 8, Attacker (resource required) 6, Wonder 6, Irrigation 4, No disease 4, Trade over sea 4, Bridges 2, Double wealth 2, Map trades 2, Communications trade 2, Small wonder 2, Specialist 2, Unit (no A/D/NT) 2, Improvement 2, Other tech 1, Recycling 1, Precision bombing 1, Worker job 1, Bonus tech 0, Reveal map 0, Sacrifices 0. "Optional: divide by 1.5".
- **Worked checks.** "Iron Working allows swordsmen and iron, so it has a value of 22 more than if it were just an empty tech". That is Resource 16 + Attacker-needing-resource 6. And "for the Romans who build Legionaries (attacker and defender), Iron Working is worth an additional 70 points!", which is Defender-needing-resource 70.
- **Version.** The base list dates from PTW-era play on 1.29f rules (Bamspeedy cites "Civ3 1.29f"; Qitai on page 2: "the research cost changes is effective vanilla civ3 1.29, not PTW"). The War Academy copy of the article (https://civfanatics.com/civ3/strategy/game-mechanics/what-will-the-ai-research-next/) has only the original list, ending "Empty Tech Cost: 256/turns" / "Optional: divide by 1.5". **C3C update:** alexman, https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/what-will-the-ai-research-next.45559/page-4 (#69, 21 Nov 2003) — "A small update with new values for Conquests:" "Cannot be traded: 149/turns", "Bonus tech: 0", "Reveal Map: 0", "Sacrifices: 0", and "luxury, bonus, and strategic resources have the same value (16), even if these are required by units and improvements." His #70 (1 Dec 2003) first claimed that C3C values resource-needing units like the others, then withdrew it: "Edit: Nevermind. My C3C test was flawed." So the 1.29f/PTW list plus these four entries is the C3C list.
- **"No trade: 149/turns" — resolved.** It is the **"Cannot Be Traded"** tech flag, named as such in alexman's C3C update above ("Cannot be traded: 149/turns"). The BIQ has it (`TECH.CannotBeTraded`, flag byte 2 bit 3). The comment in DevelopmentAI.cs that says "no BIQ tech flag found" is wrong. No default C3C tech sets this flag, so standard games are unaffected.
- **"Other tech: 1" — resolved.** alexman, same page (#77, 5 Nov 2004) — "A tech gets 1 point for each 'other tech' of which it is a direct prerequisite." It counts each tech that lists this one as a prerequisite. Tension: by the C3C BIQ as read with QueryCiv3, Iron Working is a prerequisite of Construction, yet alexman's early Iron Working example totals exactly 22 (16 + 6). Either the 2003 example left out the +1, or the prerequisite differed in the tree he tested. At 1 point this is negligible.
- **Flavors left out on purpose.** Same page, alexman (#77): "Flavors are more complicated than other things in the list, so I have not included them here." Ambiorix: "Since they're not used in a standard game, I haven't included them in the calculations." Default C3C techs have flavors = 0 in the BIQ.
- **How the values become a choice.** alexman, 45559 page 2 (#24): "it seems that there is a random factor involved" and "the higher the AI value of a tech, the more likely it will be chosen." Qitai (#34): "There is a RNG involve in the choosing of what the AI research." Ambiorix (#35) proposed, untested, a chance proportional to value: "Would it make sense to say that the chance of tech one being chosen would be 200/(200+50) ?" DaveMcW, from his C3C model, https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/ais-favorite-techs.123345/ — "These are the 20 favorite techs of the Conquests AI." and "The top 5 or so techs in an era will be researched at close to 100% probablility, but it gets harder to predict as you go down the list." Owner ruling for the engine: a weighted roll on these values (DevelopmentAI.cs uses linear weights). The exact weighting is not documented.
- **Worked C3C totals (Doc Tsiolkovski, same 123345 thread; player arithmetic).** "Nationalism: 259 (conscription) + 259 (mob) + 198 (defender) + 134 (attacker) + 8 (MPP) +2 (specialist) =860", "Divide by 1.5 (optional) = 573.3333333"; "Steam: 16 (Coal) + 1 (RR) + 6 (Iron Works) = 23". TimBentley corrects him: "the values for conscription and mobilization are divided by the number of turns." So the per-turn items shrink as research time grows. The model is reported "80%+ accurate at least" (Beorn-eL-Feared) against play.
- **Other nuances in the thread.** Unique units count only when they differ from the unit they replace ("Bowmen... count as both attacker and defender"). Only direct effects count (Sanitation gets nothing for Battlefield Medicine). A benefit counts even if the civ already has it (Magnetism still gets Trade over Ocean). Flavors: Ozymandias, https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/manipulating-ai-tech-research.657856/ — "I'm 99% certain that the effect of a Flavor is 5%." This is UNVERIFIED and not in DevelopmentAI.cs. Vanilla also fixed an AI bug: README-V "Fixed AI bug giving value to worthless techs."

### Scientific trait
- **Effect.** Pedia GCON_Strengths — "Scientific city improvements (like research labs) are easier to build and the civilization receives a free Civilization Advance at the start of every era." https://civfanatics.com/civ3/faq/ — "Upon entering a new age, they receive a free technology (one of the starting technologies for that age)."
- **Selection is random among the new era's researchable techs, and seems to avoid techs AIs already have.** https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/free-tech-tricks.90177 (DaveMcW) — the trick yields "a _different_ free tech that none of the AIs have"; with the Russian triple slingshot there is "a 33% chance to learn Education or Gunpowder as your scientific tech". - **Firaxis on the selection rule.** Mike Breitkreutz (Firaxis, lead programmer for PTW), quoted by Catt in https://codehappy.net/apolyton/threads/65178-1.htm (16 Jan 2003, from a CFC GOTM spoiler thread) — "It is supposed to select, at random, one tech from the new era that is available to the player" "(i.e., it has no prerequisites)." In 1.29f the pick was biased by list order: "As you can see, Rocketry is heavily favored (simply because of it's placement within the list)." His Modern-era odds were about 91% Rocketry, 7% Ecology, 1% Fission and 1% Computers. "This is unintentional and all 4 techs should have an equal chance of being selected." / "I will look into improving this for the next PTW patch." The README-PTW line above is that fix. So the intended rule (PTW and C3C) is a uniform random pick among the new era's researchable techs. DaveMcW's "a _different_ free tech that none of the AIs have" is consistent with the pick excluding techs you already hold, for example ones just traded for. Whether techs known to AIs are excluded is UNVERIFIED.
- **1.29f test.** Same thread, Catt (Oct 2002): he reloaded before the Modern Age and tried all four research choices, and "In all cases I received Ecology as the free tech." So the pick did not depend on the current research, and it was not re-rolled on reload. The thread does not explain why it was Ecology rather than the favoured Rocketry.
- **Timing.** https://civfanatics.com/?p=152 — after the "new era" popup, the free tech shows up once the turn is processed ("check your research screen").

Pedia GCON_Hurry_Production only mentions "improvements or units".
- **Switching shields between wonders is free.** https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/building-wonders.8756 (Ruffin) — "switch all the production over to the new wonder (no penalty in Civ3)". This is the standard "prebuild" (Palace or another wonder as placeholder): https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/quick-answers.117535/page-32. Apolyton tip on the timing limit: https://apolyton.net/forum/miscellaneous/archives/civ3-strategy-archive/42626-distilled-civ3-tips-and-notes?p=953406 (Travathian) — "If it gets to one turn left you can switch to other units/improvements, but NOT wonders."
- **Switching between categories (unit ↔ improvement ↔ wonder): no penalty (Firaxis manual).** MANUAL p.61 (new-features list) — "No penalty for changing projects: The penalty for changing production in mid-project is gone--except for any shields lost as overrun." MANUAL p.141 ("Note for Experienced Players") — "There is no penalty for switching production in mid-stream, unless the new project costs fewer shields than are already accumulated, in which case you forfeit the excess shields as overrun." Players agree: https://apolyton.net/forum/miscellaneous/archives/civ3-strategy-archive/57954-solution-to-avoid-micromanagement (Franses) — "Shields are carried over in case you change production to something that costs more (or less) than the shields produced." No later readme brings a penalty back. The 50% "categories" rule found online (https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/help-build-a-wonder.174050/) is from a Civ2 forum thread, so it is excluded. The limits on switching to a wonder after rushing or chopping are the ones above.
- **A rival completes the wonder you are building: switch, and the excess over the new item is lost (Firaxis manual).** MANUAL p.130 — "If you are building a Great Wonder in one of your cities and the same Wonder is completed elsewhere before you finish, you must convert your production to something else. Any excess shields are lost, so be careful what you choose." This matches the players: https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/wonder-building.27531 (Archullus) — "Switch to a less favourable wonder; or Get abortive production cost."; https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/building-wonders.8756 — after switching to a Bank the poster was "wasting about 400 shields". So the shields stay in the box, a switch to another wonder keeps them all, and a switch to something cheaper loses the excess when it completes. Vanilla also added notices: README-V "\"beginning wonder\" pop-ups now only appear if you have an embassy or spy with the corresponding civ." The "reimbursed in cash" claim (forums.civfanatics.com/threads/list-of-bugs-ive-experienced.139564) is from a Civ4/BtS thread and is excluded.
- **Obsolete wonders: the effect stops, the culture continues.** MANUAL p.130 — "The achievement of later advances can negate the benefits of older Wonders. The cultural benefits of a Wonder continue to accrue unless the Wonder is destroyed." and the glory and culture points "continue to accrue even if new advances make the Wonder's specific benefit obsolete." Players: https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/obsolete.100124/ (Red Ant) — "They will still generate culture points for you, though."; crazyluke2001 — "When it becomes obsolete it no longer has an effect." Obsolescence is per owner: gunkulator (same thread) — "In Civ3, the wonder is obsolete only if the _owner_ has the tech." Contrast Civ2: "In Civ2, the wonder would be become obsolete if _any_ civ discovered the appropriate tech." EDHELP "Rendered Obsolete By (Wonder Properties)" — "Determines which improvement (technology) renders the selected wonder obsolete (if any)." README-V: "Players now receive a message when a wonder is made obsolete." Whether an obsolete wonder still counts toward a Golden Age trigger is UNVERIFIED (Golden Age section).
- **Captured and razed wonders.** MANUAL p.130 — "If a Great Wonder is captured (along with the city it's in), its benefits go to the new owner." / "Great Wonders are not destroyed when an enemy captures the city in which they exist. However, if a city possessing one is razed, that Wonder is lost forever and cannot be rebuilt." The captor gets the effect but not the culture (players): https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/captured-great-wonders-where-are-the-culture-points.72979/ — wilbill, "When you capture a wonder you get its effects (if they're still active) but not the culture."; SuperBeaverInc., "Captured Wonders never produce culture." The culture rules belong to section 3.
- **Hurrying (Vanilla manual).** MANUAL p.130 — "If you want to complete construction of a Wonder faster than the city that is building it can generate shields, you have only one option: use a leader. There is no other method of hurrying a Wonder project." C3C limits this further; see the next item.
- **Great Leader hurry.**
  - Vanilla/PTW: military leaders could rush Great Wonders. https://civfanatics.com/civ3/faq/ — "In **Conquests** the rushing ability is limited to Small Wonders, but in **Vanilla** or **Play the World** it can rush a Great Wonder."
  - C3C, military leader: Pedia GCON_Leaders — "A Military Leader can be used to either create an army or to hurry production of an Improvement or Small Wonder in a city." Arathorn — "Military Great Leaders (MGLs) can no longer rush Great Wonders."
  - C3C, scientific leader: Arathorn — "An SGL can rush a wonder (either Great or Small)."
- **Golden Age from wonders.** Pedia GCON_Golden_Age — "If a civilization builds a Great Wonder (or Wonders) that corresponds to [both] its unique strengths, its Golden Age can be triggered." BIQ RULE Golden Age duration = 20. Details belong in the Golden Age section.
- **Coastal, river and resource requirements** (BIQ). Colossus, Great Lighthouse and Magellan's need a coastal city (CoastalInstallation). Hoover Dam needs a river (MustBeNearRiver). Statue of Zeus needs Ivory, Manhattan Uranium, Apollo Aluminum, and Iron Works Iron + Coal.
- **Wonder victory** (C3C option, only if enabled). README-C3C — "The "Wonder Victory" is actually a Time Limit type of victory condition; i.e., when the Wonder Victory is in effect, the game ends immediately upon completion of the last Great Wonder." Victory-point games are out of scope.

## Disputed

1. **Difficulty and research cost.** *AI side resolved:* since 1.29f the AI pays the same cost at every level and gets a compensating bonus or penalty instead. alexman (Firaxis), https://codehappy.net/apolyton/threads/66516-1.htm — "constant for all levels for the AI"; 45559 page 2 #31 — "The AI gets a production bonus (or penalty) which exactly cancels the increase (or decrease) in cost". The FAQ's Chieftain line ("is 2 for the AI – meaning everything takes twice as long to do, from researching a tech, to building a worker") describes that net effect, or the pre-1.29f rule ("twice as expensive for the AI", alexman). *Human side still disputed below Regent:* mydisease's 1.29f thread caps CF at 10 ("CF has a maximum value of 10"), so a human's cost would never fall below Regent. alexman says "since 1.29f, Chieftain technologies cost half as much for the player", which is 10/20 with no cap. Owner ruling for the engine: cap at 10 for humans. Above Regent the sources agree (x10/CF; confirmed in C3C by AutomatedTeller's Emperor Pottery = 60 and tjs282's "1.25x").
2. **Minimum research time.** 4 turns (BIQ RULE; EDHELP "Default is 4"; FAQ "For all three games, the minimum research length is 4 turns"; David Weldon; korn469's tech-rate-1 test) vs Special_K, https://apolyton.net/forum/miscellaneous/archives/civ3-strategy-archive/42626-distilled-civ3-tips-and-notes?p=953406 — "You can research an advance in 3 turns, it just takes a ton of science." The 3-turn cases reported in https://codehappy.net/apolyton/threads/33948-2.htm involve output rising, or the cost falling, part-way through (4.2). Best reading: the floor is set when research starts, and later changes can beat it by a turn. The maximum is 32, then 40, in Vanilla (README-V 1.16f) and 50 in C3C (BIQ/FAQ).
3. **Overflow.** "all extra beakers are lost" (Rik Meleet; FAQ "no beaker carryover"; Jim's and T-hawk's 2002 tests) vs "excess from the completing city is lost, later cities' beakers go to the next tech" (TimTheEnchanter / ElephantU, city order = reverse founding order). The second is more specific and consistent with the first as a simplification.
4. **Scientific leader chance.** *Mostly resolved for 3% / 5%:* Arathorn; Turner (https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/great-leader-abilities.88505/latest); socralynnek, "hardcoded to be 3% for non-scientific civs and 5% for scientific civs"; AutomatedTeller (211436); the 2008 "2 x Sci Leaders" arithmetic (0.03 x 0.03, 0.05 x 0.05). The outliers are Ginger_Ale, https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/leaders.102561 — 2% for non-scientific civs — and Sullla (a C3C beta tester), https://sullla.com/Civ3/conquestsed.html — "there is a 2-5% chance that a leader forms". No Firaxis number; the Pedia says only "increased slightly".
5. **Can a C3C military leader rush anything?** Pedia, FAQ and Arathorn: yes, improvements and Small Wonders. EdSupp: "NOTE: Military leaders can not rush." HellboundHeathcliff, https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/great-leader-abilities.88505/latest — "Conquests MGLs can only rush improvements and small wonders or create an army." EdSupp is the outlier.
6. **Theory of Evolution techs.** Current research plus next pick (Yndy, Catt 1.21f; RebelX210, "will finish up the tech you are currently researching and give you one more"; Bella Hella, "you can choose the techs you want after getting ToE") vs random (philippe, https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/how-does-the-great-library-work.26689 — "thoery of evolution picks random techs"; EDHELP "Two free, randomly selected advances are granted"). Turner describes three techs in one well-timed turn (current research, then two picks). The player reports are concrete and agree with each other. The editor wording may describe the flag loosely.
7. **Wonder data from the CivFanatics wonder guide** (https://civfanatics.com/?p=151, as extracted by WebFetch; not re-verified line by line) differs from the BIQ on Copernicus' Observatory (Invention vs BIQ Astronomy). It also lists Universal Suffrage under "Industrialisation" and spells several names differently. The BIQ is authoritative for C3C 1.22.
8. ~~Shields after losing a wonder race~~ — *resolved* by the manual (MANUAL p.130, "you must convert your production to something else. Any excess shields are lost"); see 4.11.
9. **Beakers on a research switch, or when you receive the tech you are researching (Vanilla-era sources only).**
   - Voluntary switch: all lost (MANUAL p.123, "you lose all of their work on the advance you order them to abandon"; Sir Yelof, Tassadar, regoarrarr) vs half kept (T-hawk, https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/science-slider-when-1-turn-from-tech-change-it-or-not.24350/ — "if you do it voluntarily, half of your beaker count applies to the new tech", and "if you switch again your accumulated research is halved again").
   - The researched tech arrives by trade, hut or the Great Library: beakers lost (David Weldon, https://codehappy.net/apolyton/threads/33948-1.htm — accumulated beakers "irrevocably lost" if you get the tech another way; MustPost, 33948-2, "I wasted all of the beakers") vs kept (eighty, 24350 page 2 — "if you get a tech from goody hut, trade, great library or theory of evol, you get to keep your beakers", with "(I hope, someone correct me if I am wrong)").
   - No C3C test found. The cost formula's "- Research done so far" term shows that stored beakers count against the tech they were spent on.
