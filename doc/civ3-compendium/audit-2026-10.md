# Engine audit against the compendium, October 2026

Each compendium section was compared with the engine code. The sourced discrepancies were fixed, with a
test each and the source quoted in a code comment. This file lists what the owner ruled on afterwards
and what is still open.

## Owner's rulings (2026-10-20), now in the code

1. **Industrious city tile:** follows the Civilopedia, so the extra shield applies in cities and
   metropolises.
2. **Resource depletion:** left as it is, so only resources in use run out.
3. **Pop rush:** follows Civ3's text. Each sacrificed citizen produces 20 shields, and enough are taken
   to finish the item (the count rounds up).
4. **AI luxury slider:** tried in paired AI trials. AIs that never used it scored lower, with more
   disorder, so the AI keeps the slider. The `no-ai-luxury` switch remains in `AiExperiments`.
5. **AI government changes at war:** the AI may revolt mid-war when war weariness or a long war presses
   it, favouring governments without war weariness. This is the default now; trials were within noise.
6. **Great Library:** one pass in editor order, with no era limit. A captured Library can take its new
   owner past Education, as far as the other civs have gone.
7. **Foreign citizens:** they never assimilate, but a starving city loses them first.
8. **AI gold per turn:** an AI never pays gold per turn beyond its surplus. Under Despotism it puts its
   surplus into research.

## Also done

- **Small fixes:**
  - AI start units are granted on the first turn the AI has a city.
  - The 8×8 resistance table now comes from the BIQ.
  - Hoover Dam and the Hydro Plant need a river anywhere in the city radius.
  - Iron Works needs Iron and Coal in its radius. The BIQ improvement flag for this was not being read.
  - A build finishing next turn can't be switched to a wonder.
  - Units join an army only in the city that made it.
- **Previously skipped items:**
  - TheNiceOne's AI–human trade markup by difficulty, aggression and attitude. Demigod and Sid are
    extrapolated (UNVERIFIED).
  - Rate of fire against cities, walls and improvements. Precision strikes still fire one shot
    (UNVERIFIED).
  - Hut and camp messages use Civ3's script.txt wording, with tribe names from the barbarian list.
- **Missing features:**
  - Fascism's Forced Resettlement and Xenophobic rules.
  - Riots: a city kept in disorder may lose an improvement.
- **Sea trade:** the deal screen greys out resources when no trade route links the two capitals.

## Still open

- **Fascism conquest losses.** The Civilopedia says a Fascist civ's "Newly conquered cities also lose
  population", but gives no amount.
- **Riot target.** Which improvement a riot destroys is unsourced. One is picked at random, never a wonder
  or the palace.
- **Long disorder.** Long disorder overthrowing the government is not implemented.
- **Map and combat:** global warming, mobilization, bombardment making craters, plague, barricades
  blocking movement, and colonies overrun by barbarians.
- **Espionage and diplomacy:** the "carefully" and "safely" spy missions, AI sabotage and propaganda, AI
  loans, and the smaller attitude terms.
