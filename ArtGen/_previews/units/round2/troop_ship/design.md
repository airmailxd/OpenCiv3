# Troop Ship, round 2

## What it is
The mod's picture (its Galleon folder) is a long one-funnel steamer with derricks and blue hatches. The model
is a WWII **attack transport** (an APA of the Haskell class, 1944): long grey hull, raised forecastle, cruiser
stern; a three-tier house amidships; one stout funnel; LCVP landing craft in davits along the house and
stacked on the hatches; paired king posts with cargo booms; a 5-inch gun aft, a 3-inch forward, 40 mm tubs.

## What must read at 1x
1. A long low hull with one central block of superstructure and a single funnel (civ band).
2. Civ-coloured hatches fore and aft under king posts and booms.
3. A row of small boats along the house's sides (the landing craft): what makes it a troop ship.

## Stock neighbours
- **Transport**: tarp-covered cargo, civ hatch patches, a bridge. Takes: civ hatches.
- **Destroyer**: turrets, a lattice mast with radar, funnel, deck clutter. Takes: the steel palette,
  the lattice mast, the gun turrets.

## Parts (Destroyer ~45, Transport ~20; target 100+)
Hull with strakes and boot top, forecastle deck and breakwater, 24 portholes; house in 3 tiers with caps,
~60 portholes/windows, bridge wings; lattice mast with radar; funnel with civ band; 2 cowl vents; 8 LCVPs in
16 davit arms, 2 LCVPs on hatches; 4 civ hatches with coamings and battens; 2 king-post pairs with
crosstrees, 4 booms, topping lifts and falls; 5-inch gun on its tub, 3-inch gun, 4 twin 40 mm tubs;
hawse pipes; jack and ensign; forestay and backstay.

## Rounds
- **r0 (round 1 as rejected):** a grey hull, boxes for a house, flat hatches.
- **r1:** the APA built as above. Wrong: the whole ship one light grey, hull and house and deck alike:
  no figure-ground; the LCVPs lost against the house.
- **r2:** Measure 22 paint: a navy blue hull, haze grey house, a dark deck. Wrong: the hull still read grey,
  the forecastle a light slab.
- **r3 (with Transport, Destroyer):** a deeper navy, the forecastle in deck colour. Figure-ground now right:
  dark hull, light house, civ hatches. Richer than both neighbours.
- **r4 (close-up):** the forecastle and the deck by the hatches are flat empty fields.
- **r5:** cargo winches at every hatch, bitts and the anchor windlass on the forecastle with its cables, life
  rafts on the house. Fields broken up by real fittings.
- **r6 (with stocks):** holds. Attack: the 5-inch and 3-inch train and fire. Death: explosions, sinking.
