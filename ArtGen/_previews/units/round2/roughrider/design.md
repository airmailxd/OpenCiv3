# Roughrider, round 2

## What it is

A trooper of the 1st US Volunteer Cavalry, the "Rough Riders", Cuba 1898. From the regiment's photographs
(Tampa and San Juan, 1898) and the Army's 1898 field kit: a drab slouch hat with a dented crown and a wide brim, a
dark blue flannel pullover shirt (a placket with buttons, two breast pockets, sleeves rolled or buttoned), a
polka-dot neckerchief, brown canvas trousers, khaki canvas leggings laced up the side over brown shoes, a Mills
woven cartridge belt full of loops, braces, leather gauntlets, a revolver holster on the right hip and the
.30-40 Krag carbine. The horse in the McClellan saddle (black leather, open-topped tree, hooded wooden stirrups),
a folded dark blue saddle blanket with an orange stripe, a blanket roll strapped across the pommel, saddlebags
behind the cantle, a canteen, an empty carbine boot on the right.

Round 1: a long-sleeved blue torso, a hat, a kerchief, khaki legs and a plain saddle. A generic cowboy. The civ
colour only on the hatband and kerchief (much less than the Cossack's or Cavalry's).

## What must read at 1x

1. **The slouch hat**: a wide, soft brim on a dented crown, the one shape that says "Rough Rider".
2. **The shirt in the civ colour** (their blue shirts) with the cartridge belt and braces breaking it.
3. **The carbine held up from the thigh**, its dark barrel against the sky.
4. **The field-kit horse**: the blanket roll horseshoe over the pommel, the saddlebags, the hooded stirrups and the
   folded blanket with its stripe.

## Neighbours

- **Cavalry** (stock, 19th c.): dragoon helmet, civ-coloured coat, carbine across the saddle, buckskin horse,
  dark saddle cloth with a light edge. Take: proportions, rider scale, the saddle cloth's size.
- **Cossack** (stock, the art this unit replaced): papakha, long coat, rifle, dark gray horse. Take: the size on
  the tile (calibration is against it), the rifle held from the thigh.

## Civ colour

The shirt (sleeves included) and the saddle blanket's body; stock Cavalry tints its coat and crest, so this is
about the same.

## Parts list (Cavalry: 52 horse parts, 48 rider; aim higher)

Horse: the stock horse (bay, black points, a blaze); McClellan saddle (seat, open slot, pommel, cantle, side bars,
skirts), cinch, hooded stirrups (wooden hoods) on leathers, folded blanket (civ body, orange stripe near its edge),
blanket roll (canvas, horseshoe over the pommel, three straps), saddlebags (two pouches with flaps and buckles),
canteen (round, cloth cover, strap), carbine boot (empty, leather, on the right behind the leg), US bit with brass
bosses, breast strap, reins.

Rider: slouch hat (dented crown, wide brim, pinched front, band), short hair, moustache, shirt (civ: body,
sleeves, placket, collar, two pockets with flaps, buttons), neckerchief (knotted, tails), braces (two straps over
the shoulders, crossed at the back), Mills cartridge belt (woven, loops all round, brass buckle), holster with a
revolver butt, gauntlets (flared cuffs), canvas trousers, laced leggings (with lacing), shoes, spurs, Krag
carbine (stock, wrist, receiver with the box magazine bulge, barrel band, barrel, sling ring).

## Look rounds

### Round 1 (r01.png)
Seen: the slouch hat reads from every side, the blue shirt is the civ colour, saddlebags and blanket stripe show.
Wrong: tan patches on the upper arms (the long-sleeved top stops above the elbow; skin showed through) and puffy
deltoids from the muscular build; the hooded stirrups came out as black balls by the horse's shoulders; the
blanket roll was hidden under the rider's thighs. Change: full-length shirt sleeves; hoods as open leather cups
round the toe; the roll moved forward onto the withers, its ends hanging in front of the knees.

### Round 2 (r02.png)
Seen: sleeves fixed, hoods read as stirrups, the roll shows as a pale horseshoe in front of the rider. Wrong: the
thighs were pale (the muscular build's quads in skin colour push through the trousers). Change: drop the muscular
build (as the stock Cavalry).

### Round 3 (r03.png)
Seen: brown trousers above pale leggings, the shirt with its placket, pockets and buttons at 2x. Wrong: the carbine
stood straight up against the body and vanished at 1x; the braces read as gold trim. Change: carbine tilted forward
and out from the thigh (w_main -16/18), braces in dark leather, thinner.

### Round 4 (r04.png)
Seen: the carbine now stands clear of the body in the SW, S and W views as a dark line; braces read as straps.

### Round 5 (a_RUN, a_ATTACK1, a_DEATH)
The gallop keeps the kit on the horse; the shot brings the carbine to the shoulder, the hat brim tilting with the
head; in the death the rider is thrown back, the hat stays on (it is part of the head), the carbine lands beside
him. Reviewer note from another group checked: the carbine isn't flat against the body, there's no gear beside the
head, the rider's body is the stock rider body at the Cossack's scale (1.0 between Cavalry 0.91 and Cossack 1.06).

### Round 6 (r06.png)
At 1x beside the Cavalry and Cossack: same rider size and horse size, the same lighting and contour; told apart by
the wide brim above the blue shirt, the bay with its kit (pale roll ends at the shoulders, dark saddlebags). The
civ colour (shirt, blanket) is about the Cavalry's coat area.

## Round 2b (after the picture-only review failed it)

Review: the hat about 1.4x shoulder width hid the face (sombrero); neckerchief, leggings and belt lost at 1x; a
flat blue shirt; a thin carbine; a dark gun-like object at the saddle back (the empty carbine boot) and a khaki tube
at the shoulder (the blanket roll's end) reading as a detached limb; aiming through the brim; in the death his legs
sink into the horse and he lies across it in a T-pose (the stock Cavalry's death does the same).

### Round 7 (r07.png)
Hat: brim radius 0.33 -> 0.235 (about 30% smaller), a fore-and-aft crease pressed into the crown, the left brim
pinned up to the crown with a brass pin, worn pushed back 9 deg so the face and collar show. Neckerchief red,
bigger (contrasts with any civ colour). Mills belt wider and lighter with bigger cartridge tips; leggings lighter
with a turned top. A canvas haversack strap across the chest to a bag on the left hip breaks the shirt. Carbine
1.16 m and 1.35x thicker. Carbine boot removed; the blanket roll moved behind the cantle, across the horse, with
rolled ends and three straps. Seen: face under the brim, red kerchief, strap and belt read; nothing detached.

### Round 8 (b_ATTACK1)
The shot: with the smaller brim pushed back the carbine comes to the shoulder under the brim, not through it.

### Round 9 (b_DEATH, first try)
The new rider death (shared with the knight): seen in E the rider still lay behind the horse's body (overlapping
in that view). Measured the fallen horse's footprint and moved his landing spot clear of it.

### Round 10 (b_DEATH in S, E, N, W)
Rider lands well clear of the horse in all four views, on his back, one knee up, arms at his sides; the carbine
lands apart. Measured: civ 15.3% (Cavalry 16.3, Cossack 10.3), sprite 3391 px (Cavalry 3472).

### Round 11 (final.png; meas1x.py)

(90.2 / 75.7): inside the stock range; no change needed.
