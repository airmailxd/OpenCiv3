# Wildcat, round 2

## What it is
The **Grumman F4F-4 Wildcat** (1942), the mod's Fighter: a stubby barrel of a fuselage with a deep belly (the
main wheels folded into its sides), a big round cowling with the twin-row engine inside, a framed greenhouse
canopy set high with small windows behind, square-tipped mid-set wings with three .50s in each leading edge,
a squared tailplane and a tall rounded fin. Blue-grey over light grey; the US star insignia.

## What must read at 1x
1. Stubby, deep fuselage behind a big round cowl (not the stock Fighter's slim Spitfire).
2. Square-tipped straight wings.
3. The insignia: a civ-coloured disc with a white star and white bars, on the wings and the fuselage.

## Stock neighbours
- **Fighter** (Spitfire-like): elliptical wings, camouflage, roundels. Takes: prop treatment, scale.
- **Bomber**: roundels, tail band. Takes: marking scale.

## Parts (Fighter ~14; target 45+)
Fuselage with 4 frame lines, belly fairing, 2 main wheels with hubs; cowl, civ lip, engine face with 14
cylinders, cowl flaps (slatted), prop hub; 4 exhaust stubs; canopy with 4 hoops and 2 bars; spine; 2 rear
windows; 2 fuselage insignia; antenna mast and wire; tail hook; tail wheel; 2 wings with 3 chordwise lines,
aileron and flap lines, 2 insignia, 6 gun barrels, pitot; 2 tailplanes with elevator lines; fin with rudder
line; 3-blade prop with yellow tips.

## Rounds
- **r0 (round 1 as rejected):** random camouflage blotches (noise: Wildcats wore plain two-tone), small dots
  for roundels, no frames, guns or panels.
- **r1:** rebuilt as above. Wrong: grey reads more than blue; the white bars outweigh the civ disc; the rear
  fuselage too slim for a Wildcat.
- **r2:** bluer paint, a fuller rear fuselage, bigger discs with shorter bars. Reads at 1x as a stubby
  blue fighter with star insignia, clearly not the stock Fighter.
- **r3 (with Fighter, Bomber):** same size as the Fighter, same contour and lighting. Holds.
- **r4 (close-up):** seen from behind the wing tops are a broad flat blue: the panel lines too faint.
- **r5:** darker panel lines (ailerons, flaps, spar lines now read at 2x). Next to the stock's muted greys the
  blue was a little loud.
- **r6:** paint desaturated a step (still the 1942 blue-grey). Attack: tracers from the wing guns.
  Death: blown apart, pieces fall to a fireball. Run: bank and bob, prop turning.

## After the first review (failed)
- **r7:** the barrel body: fuselage and tail scaled 0.9 long, 1.1 wide, 1.08 deep (the wings keep their
  span); the light under-colour carried up the fuselage sides; a dark walkway on each wing root; a civ band
  round the rear fuselage; the insignia bars shortened. Framing and death as the P-38 (size 0.88, drop 4,
  `aircraft_r2`).
- **r8-r12 (measured):** the blue-grey paint was being counted as civ blue (55%!): the paint is now the
  greyer FS 35189 tone, the cowl lip no longer civ, the insignia smaller: civ blue 6.0% (Fighter 6.3,
  Bomber 3.1), brightness 101.5 (95-104), area within the Fighter's.
