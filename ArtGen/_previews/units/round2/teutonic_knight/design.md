# Teutonic Knight, round 2

## What it is

A knight-brother of the Teutonic Order (Deutscher Orden) in the 13th-14th century, the Baltic crusades: the
Order's white mantle with the black cross, a white surcoat over mail, a flat-topped great helm (Topfhelm) with a
crest, a white heater shield with the black cross, a couched-ready lance; the destrier under a full white cloth
caparison (head to croup) with black crosses. Reference: the Order's dress rules (white mantle for knight-brothers
only), the Codex Manesse-era helm crests, the knights of Eisenstein's *Alexander Nevsky* for the look on the field.

Round 1 was the stock Knight's horse with a white blanket and box crosses floating off the cloth, a plain helm
with a flat blue fan, a tunic, and a small shield. It read as "a knight, recoloured".

## What must read at 1x

1. **White on white with black crosses**: the mantle and the caparison are the unit. Crosses are the Order's cross
   (straight arms, flared a little at the ends), lying *on* the cloth (painted into the cloth mesh, never floating).
2. **The mantle**: a long white cloak from the shoulders falling behind over the horse's croup: a shape no stock
   knight has.
3. **The great helm with a crest**: a flat-topped pot helm with a brass cross on its face, and a pair of tall
   civ-colored wings standing on it (the V silhouette above the head).
4. **The lance with a swallow-tailed civ-colored pennon**, the heater shield with the cross on the left arm.

## Neighbours

- **Knight** (stock): steel barding with civ-colored skirts and gold hems, great helm, surcoat, oval shield, sword.
  Take: the horse, its scale, the steel chanfron, the saddle height, the metal palette (#9a9ca2 steel, mail
  #7a7c80), the amount of civ colour (skirt + surcoat + emblem: about a fifth of the sprite).
- **Sipahi** (stock): a cloth caparison with a pattern, chanfron and neck steel. Take: how a patterned caparison
  reads, the gold-trimmed hem.

## Civ colour

The stock Knight's tint is large. Here white and black must stay the Order's, so the civ colour goes on things a
knight of the Order did carry in colour: the crest wings, a broad hem band round the caparison (and neck cloth),
the pennon, the reins and the shield's guige. Target about the Knight's area minus the surcoat.

## Parts list (Knight: 52 horse parts, 45 rider; aim higher, each a real thing)

Horse: the stock horse; a caparison of my own (fine grid; white body, crosses on both flanks and on the chest,
broad civ hem band with a thin black edge, scalloped hem lifting between the legs); a cloth crinet over the neck
with its own hem band; steel chanfron with a ridge and a small spike; brow band and reins (civ); a high war
saddle (tall curved cantle, high pommel plate); stirrup leathers and iron stirrups; a breast strap edge showing
at the chest.

Rider: great helm (bowl, flat top plate, eye slits, a brass cross on the face, breaths), wing crest (two wings,
each with three feather lobes), mail coif collar showing below the helm, white surcoat (chest and back crosses,
split skirt hanging both sides of the saddle), white mantle (shoulder yoke, long back panel over the croup,
cross on the left breast), sword belt with a long sword in its scabbard at the left hip, mail sleeves and
chausses, steel knee cops (poleyns), mail mittens, spurs, heater shield (curved face, black cross, steel rim,
guige strap), lance (shaft, grip, vamplate, head, pennon).

## Look rounds

(below, one entry per round)

### Round 1 (r01.png)
Seen: the white caparison and crinet with cross pattee on the flanks and the civ hem read at once, at 1x too; the
crosses lie on the folds. Wrong: the helm's wings came out white (the crest material wasn't in the tint list) and
looked like rabbit ears; the mantle was a white bell with a straight bottom edge, a flat box behind the rider in
the side and back views (a flat-white-area tell); the guige strap was civ-coloured and read as a blue line across
the chest. Change: tint the crest; reshape the wings (root low, leading edge curving out, five primaries stepped
down the trailing edge, splayed 24 deg); rebuild the mantle as a cape whose hem drops lowest at the back and rises
to the front edges, with nine pleats deepening toward the hem; guige in leather; folds on the caparison deeper.

### Round 2 (r02.png)
Seen: the mantle now hangs as a pleated cape and reads as cloth; wings read as a V from the front, a plume-like
shape from the side; at 1x the unit is told from the Knight by white + black crosses + wings at a glance. Size on
the tile matches the Knight's horse. Wrong: hem band a little thin against the Knight's civ area. Change: hem band
0.15 m (was 0.13), black edge line above it.

### Round 3 (a_RUN, a_ATTACK1, a_DEATH)
Animations checked in S and E: the gallop keeps the cloth on the barrel and the legs clear below the hem; the
thrust reads with the lance level at the strike; in the death the horse falls on its side with its caparison, the
rider is thrown and the lance (with its pennon) lands beside them. Nothing pokes through the cloth that the stock
Knight doesn't also show.

### Round 4 (r04.png)
Seen from the back (N) and NE: the mantle's back and the caparison's croup were two blank white areas. Change: a
cross over the croup behind the saddle (caparisons of the Order carried them there too), pleats on the mantle
deeper and more of them (11).

### Round 5 (r05.png)
Seen: the croup cross breaks the back view, the mantle shades as cloth. Reviewer note from another group: gear
standing up beside the head reads as horns, and single primitives standing in for armour fail. The two splayed
wings were exactly that V. Change: the wings close together into one crest standing fore-and-aft on the helm (seen
from the front a narrow fan, from the side the wing); the knee cops from ellipsoids into poleyns (a cup with a
lame above and below and a fan-shaped side wing).

### Round 6 (r06.png)
Seen: the crest now reads as a crest from every side, nothing beside the head; poleyns read as plate at 2x. At 1x
among the Knight and Sipahi: same horse size, same steel palette, the civ colour on hem, crest, pennon and reins.

## Round 2b (after the picture-only review failed it)

Review: 20-23% of the tile against the stock knights' 13-17% ("a sheet over a table on stilts"), head and neck
lost, civ 11% against 16-21%, flat cloak, specks at the hem, skin at shoulders and elbows, a thrust that barely
moves with the pennon vanishing, a rider spread-eagled on the fallen horse. Checked the stock Knight's death
(stock/Knight_death.png): it has the same fault (arms over the head, rider on the horse), so the death is ours.

### Round 7 (r07.png)

white cross, so the neck reads against the white body. The mantle lined in the civ colour, its front edges turned
back. Mail spheres and full mail sleeves over the shoulders and elbows. Seen: legs, neck and head read; much
closer to the Knight's size.

### Round 9 (b_ATTACK1, b_DEATH in four directions)

(shorter, no flare, folds bunching, rebuilt per frame); the rider is thrown clear, turning in the air, and lands
on his back beside the horse, one knee up, arms at his sides; the lance lands apart. Seen in S, E, N, W: rider and
horse separate at the end in every view.

### Round 10 (r09.png)

showing at shoulders or elbows. At 1x beside the Knight and Sipahi: same horse size, white/black with civ hem,
crinet and lining.
