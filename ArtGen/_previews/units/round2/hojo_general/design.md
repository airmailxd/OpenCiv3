# Hojo General, round 2

## What it is

A commander of the Later Hojo clan of Odawara (Sengoku period, 1500s), on foot, as in the art it replaced (the
stock Army: one standing figure). Drawn from Sengoku tosei-gusoku armor in the museum collections (Odawara
Castle's) and the period screens of generals in the field: a black-lacquered okegawa-do (horizontal plates, rows
of gold rivet heads) over a dark under-robe, big sode, kusazuri of laced lames, kote sleeves with plates, haidate,
suneate; a kabuto with a wide shikoro, tall gold kuwagata and the clan crest between them; a red-lacquered menpo
(face mask with a moustache); over it all the commander's **jinbaori**, a sleeveless campaign surcoat flaring at
the hips, with the Hojo crest, the **mitsu-uroko** (three scales: three triangles in a triangle), large on its
back; in his right hand the **gunbai**, the lacquered war fan generals directed battle with; the daisho (katana and
wakizashi) in the sash; behind him his own standard, a tall sashimono with the mitsu-uroko.

Round 1: a footman in dark armor with a sashimono and a katana. It read as "a samurai in black": nothing said
general, nothing said Hojo; dark, flat areas; the civ colour only on the flag.

## What must read at 1x

1. **The jinbaori in the civ colour** over black armor, with the pale three-scale crest on its back: the shape of a
   commander, wide at the shoulders and hips.
2. **The gunbai** held out in the right hand: a round paddle with a dark sun on it.
3. **The gold kuwagata** on the helmet and the red face mask.
4. **The sashimono with the mitsu-uroko** above him.

## Neighbours

- **Minamoto Samurai** (MDJ stock): kabuto with gold horns, civ-coloured laced armor, katana low. Take: armor
  proportions, the gold, the build.
- **Medieval Japanese Leader** (MDJ stock daimyo at court): civ-coloured mantle and panels, fan. Take: how a
  leader's civ-coloured garment covers the figure.
- (Ashigaru Spearman for the sashimono's size.)

## Civ colour

The jinbaori (most of the figure seen from behind, the shoulders and hip flares from the front) and the
sashimono's field; about the Minamoto's coverage.

## Parts list (Minamoto: 97 parts; round 1: 109; aim higher, each a real thing)

Kabuto (bowl with ridges, brim band, shikoro of 4 lames with lacing rows, fukigaeshi, kuwagata pair, crest disc
with the mon, tehen ring on top), menpo (red mask, nose, moustache, throat guard yodare-kake of lames), do
(okegawa plates, rivet rows, top plate, armpit plates), sode (lames, lacing), kote (cloth sleeve, forearm plate,
hand guard), kusazuri (7 panels, 4 lames each, lacing), haidate (thigh guard apron), suneate (plates, ties), tabi
and waraji, under-robe sleeves, hakama; jinbaori (back panel with the slit, two front panels, shoulder yoke,
collar band, flared hip skirts, the mon on the back, white trim); obi sash; katana and wakizashi in scabbards with
tsuba; gunbai (blade, rim, sun disc, handle, cord); sashimono (pole, holder, crossbar, banner, mon).

## Look rounds

### Round 1 (r01.png)
Seen: kuwagata, red menpo and the gunbai with its red sun all read; the jinbaori skirt reads in the civ colour.
Wrong: the shikoro (4 wide lames) covered the upper back and shoulders, so the jinbaori's back and its crest were
hidden and the top half was all black; the crest broke up into scattered white bits (the grid was too coarse for
it); the daisho pointed forward-down like spikes; the figure was larger than the Minamoto Samurai (fitted to the
original's banner height). Change: shikoro 3 lames, narrower; the jinbaori's body grid 21 rows; daisho turned to
point back and down; catalog scale 0.86.

### Round 2 (r02.png)
Seen: the mitsu-uroko now reads on the back as three pale triangles; the jinbaori reads as a surcoat over black
armor; size on the tile matches the Minamoto Samurai. Wrong: the front views are mostly black lacquer; the scabbards
cross the jinbaori skirt in the back view.

### Round 3 (r03.png)
Change tried: a gilt crest on the do, the front opening narrowed to 30 deg, daisho turned outward. Seen: the daisho
now stuck straight out sideways (worse); the gunbai held across the chest hid the do; the do's crest didn't show.

### Round 4 (r04.png)
Change: the gunbai held out beside the right shoulder (reads as a fan beside the body, not over it); daisho back
toward the rear. Seen: the do front was one flat black area: neither the crest nor the gilt plate lines showed.
Found by debug renders with gear removed: the crest plate sat inside the do (it ignored the chest rings' forward
offset) and the do's plate lines shared the red 'lacing' material. Change: crest placed on the do's surface; the
do's lines in their own gilt material.

### Round 5 (r05.png, a_RUN, a_ATTACK1, a_DEATH)
Seen: the gilt mitsu-uroko on the black do, gilt plate lines, the white sash below: the front is broken up. The
katana is drawn for the slash and the gunbai stays out of it; run carries the fan; the death falls back cleanly
(the rigid skirt lies flat between the legs, as the stock skirts do). Wrong: the scabbards still reached out
sideways in the SW and W views.

### Round 6 (r06.png)
Change: daisho hang steeper (tips back and down along the skirt). Seen: no gear sticking out; at 1x among the
Minamoto Samurai and the daimyo it reads as a commander: civ-coloured surcoat with the crest, gold kuwagata, red
mask, round fan. Size matches the Minamoto.
