# Pirate, round 2

## What it is
The mod's own picture (its Man-O-War folder) is a steam junk, so the genre model is a 19th-century South
China Sea pirate's steam junk (the Pearl River's armed lorchas and paddle junks): a low grey iron hull with a
ram bow and a planked deck, paddle wheels amidships, a plated casemate aft, one tall funnel, a gun turret
forward, and a junk rig: fan-battened sails whose battens radiate from the foot of the luff.

## What must read at 1x
1. The fan sails: rays of civ colour and cloth, scalloped between their battens (not flat cards).
2. The paddle boxes: a round drum with fanned vents and a civ-coloured rim at the waterline amidships.
3. Low grey hull with a dark boot top, a black funnel, a casemate aft.

## Stock neighbours
- **Privateer**: square-rigged with billowed sails, a dark hull, shrouds and flags. Takes: sail volume and
  rigging density, the flag at the masthead.
- **Ironclad**: grey steel, turret, civ plates. Takes: the steel palette and the turret.

## Parts (Privateer ~60, Ironclad ~12; target 90+)
Hull with plating stripes, boot top, civ stripe, planked deck; 2 paddle drums (vents, rim, hub), sponson
walks, the shaft; casemate (plates, civ band, 12 ports), its upper cabin, wheelhouse (windows on 3 sides),
rail with stanchions; funnel with civ band, its base, 4 cowl vents, a whistle; turret on a barbette, bow
chaser; anchor; boat on 2 davits; 3 fan sails x 7 panels with 8 battens and 4 sheets each, their yards;
the tan mizzen junk sail (5 panels); 4 masts; 2 flags; forestay, masthead stays, backstay, 6 shrouds.

## Rounds
- **r0 (round 1 as rejected):** flat striped cards for sails, plain hull, a drum for paddle boxes.
- **r1:** fan sails (each panel its own bellying cloth, battens as spars, sheets fanning to a block),
  paddle drums with fanned vents, casemate with plates/ports/civ band, cowl vents, boat on davits. Wrong:
  the sails hang to one side of their masts in the S/N views (the mod's are centred).
- **r2:** sails re-centred on their masts; a wheelhouse with windows on the casemate.
- **r3 (with Privateer, Ironclad):** holds in all 8 views; rigging density matches the Privateer. Wrong: half of
  every sail is civ colour, far more than the Privateer's emblems (the mod's picture is that blue, but the
  stock ships around it are not).
- **r4:** civ rays on every other panel only, the plain panels tipped with it at the rim (about 40% of the
  cloth). Close-ups (r4z): paddle drum, casemate ports, cowl vents, boat and wheelhouse all read; the sails
  belly between battens in every view, no card.
- **r5 (with stocks):** holds. Attack: the turret trains to starboard, flash and smoke. Death: the shared
  sinking with explosions (modern). Run: bob, wake.
