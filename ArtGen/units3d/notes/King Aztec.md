# King Aztec
- Pose: standing feet apart, both arms crossed over the bare chest.
- Gear: tall gold headdress with an upright fan of feathers (gold, civ color, red); bare torso; long shoulder cape/cloak edged in gold; teal-green loincloth/kilt; sandals. From behind the cape top is gold with vertical civ-color and teal fringed panels and a red square block near the bottom.
- Colors: skin brown (~#a07a58), gold (~#e0c040), teal-green (~#4a9080), red (~#b02020).
- Tinted (civ color): the cape (front edges and vertical back panels) and parts of the headdress band/feathers.
- Versus old remake: mostly matches; cape back is gold-topped with fringed panels and a red block, not plain; headdress is a tall upright fan, not a leaning crest.
