# Medieval Infantry
- Pose: square stance; right hand holds a flail low at the side, chain and spiked ball dangling near the knee; left arm carries a shield at the side.
- Gear: closed steel helmet (great helm style with eye slit); full chain mail on arms and legs; a long surcoat/tabard from shoulders to ankles over the mail with a brown belt at the waist; tan wooden kite/heater-ish oval shield; pale steel sabatons.
- Colors: steel/mail gray (#84848c to #b4b4bc), brown belt, tan wood shield (#a88a5c), gold trim at the surcoat hem, white-silver feet.
- Tinted (civ color): the long surcoat, front and back, from shoulders to ankles (with a gold hem edge).
- Versus old remake: catalog 'tunic' + 'kilt' is too short; the tinted surcoat reaches the ankles. Helmet is a closed great helm, not just a mail coif; shield is a tall tan oval, not a small round.
