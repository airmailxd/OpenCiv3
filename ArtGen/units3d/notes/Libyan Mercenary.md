# Libyan Mercenary
- Pose: upright stance; right hand holds a long spear vertically, butt on the ground, point well above the head; left arm carries a large round shield held at the side, face outward.
- Gear: gold/bronze Attic helmet with a crest; white-gray linen/scale cuirass over a white knee-length tunic with a dark belt; bronze greaves over brown leg wraps; round white shield with a gold rim.
- Colors: bronze-gold helmet and greaves (#b8a068), silver-gray cuirass (#a2a2a6), off-white tunic and shield (#e6e2d8), brown skin, dark spear head.
- Tinted (civ color): the short sleeves/upper arms, a small hem band at the bottom of the tunic, and the star/sunburst emblem on the shield.
- Versus old remake: catalog tints 'top' (whole tunic) and colors the sleeves off-white; in the original only the sleeves (plus tunic hem) are civ color and the tunic body is white.
