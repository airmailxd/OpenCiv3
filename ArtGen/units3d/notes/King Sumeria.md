# King Sumeria
- Pose: upright, holds a long thin staff/rod horizontally across the body at chest-waist height, right hand forward, rod tip pointing out to the side.
- Gear: tall white conical crown with gold band/top; long black hair and long square black beard; cream-white shawl with red/brown patterning wrapped over chest and one shoulder with red band at the waist; long ankle-length skirt; gold-and-black striped panel at side and hem; red border bands; bare feet/sandals.
- Colors: blue skirt, cream shawl (#e4dccc), red bands (#8a3226), gold/black striped hem (#d8a820/#2a1a10), black hair, brown skin, wooden rod.
- Tinted (civ color): the long skirt (front and back, with vertical fold stripes) and the short cap sleeves at the shoulders.
- Versus old remake: catalog skirt is brown with only thin bands tinted - in the original the skirt itself is blue; staff is held horizontally, not as an upright mace-scepter; shawl over the torso is missing.
