# War Elephant

These notes come from the original Civ3 art (DEFAULT, 8 directions).

**The elephant.** A big tan-brown Indian elephant, heavily wrinkled all over:
- deep folds on the legs and flanks, and horizontal ring creases down the trunk;
- the trunk hangs straight to the ground between the tusks;
- large, rounded ears lie back against the neck;
- the head is domed;
- the legs are thick columns with broad round feet and pale toenails;
- a thin tail ends in a dark tuft.

**The tusks.** Long, white and curving down and forward. Each has a civ-colored band near its root and a long gold cap on the lower half of the tusk, ending in a point.

**The forehead disc.** A silver-white disc in a gold ring on the brow.

**The caparison.** A civ-colored cloth over the back, falling down both flanks to below the belly line. It has a gold border with a Greek-key (meander) pattern and gold corner ornaments. Dark straps (girth and breast band) hold it on.

**The howdah.** A dark wooden tower on the back with plank sides, under a flat civ-colored canopy roof edged in gold. The roof carries a gold key-pattern frame.

**Shields and spears.** A large round silver shield with a gold rim on each side of the howdah. Several spears with gold-brown shafts and steel heads stick out forward from the howdah, above the head.

**No mahout or crew** is visible.

**Colors.** The hide is tan-grey with warm highlights. The civ color is on the caparison, the canopy and the tusk bands. Gold is on the borders, the tusk caps, the disc ring and the shield rims.
