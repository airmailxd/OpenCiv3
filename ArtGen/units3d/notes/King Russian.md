# King Russian
- Pose: stately, seated-looking in an enormous bell gown; right hand holds a short gold scepter raised upright beside the shoulder; left hand rests in front at the lap holding a small gold object (orb).
- Gear: small silver-and-gold crown on brown hair in a bun; fitted bodice with gold front panel and white lace; puffy grey-white lace sleeves; very wide, almost cylindrical hoop skirt with grey-silver brocade pattern and white lace frill at the hem.
- Colors: blue gown dominated, silver-grey brocade (#a0a4ac), white lace (#e8e8e8), gold scepter/front panel (#c8a030), pale skin.
- Tinted (civ color): most of the gown - skirt base color under the silver brocade, the bodice and the front skirt panel; sleeves and lace stay grey-white.
- Versus old remake: catalog makes the gown grey with only a sash and stripes tinted - in the original the skirt and bodice are mostly blue with grey brocade on top; there is no visible sash.
