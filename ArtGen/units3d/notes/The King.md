# The King
- Pose: wide-legged, knees bent rock-and-roll stance; arms spread out to the sides at waist height holding the cape open; right hand holds a microphone.
- Gear: black pompadour, sunglasses; white jumpsuit with high collar and deep V; gold belt; gold cuffs; wide bell-bottom trousers; short cape from shoulders to knees.
- Colors: white jumpsuit (#f4f2ee), gold belt/cuffs (#d8a830), black hair and mic.
- Tinted (civ color): the cape, the V chest panels and the inserts in the bell-bottom flares.
