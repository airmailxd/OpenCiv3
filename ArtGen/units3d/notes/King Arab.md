# King Arab
- Pose: standing upright, forearms folded/crossed in front of the waist; calm.
- Gear: large round turban; full black beard; long loose cream robe to the ankles with wide sleeves; a sash/baldric running diagonally from shoulder to opposite hip, with a gold stripe along it; brown sandals or slippers.
- Colors: robe off-white cream (~#e0d8c4), sash civ color with gold stripe, skin tan-brown, beard dark.
- Tinted (civ color): turban, the diagonal sash, sleeve cuffs, robe front edge trims and the hem band.
- Versus old remake: catalog has the sash as a waist sash; in the original it is a diagonal shoulder-to-hip band with a gold stripe. Otherwise matches.
