# Immortals
- Pose: upright, legs apart; curved scimitar in the right hand, low and swept out down/back away from the body.
- Gear: white cloth cap/hood with a veil wrapping the chin and mouth; tan tunic with a dark brown diagonal strap (baldric) front and back; sash at the waist; baggy trousers; gold bracelets; yellow shoes.
- Colors: white headcloth (#ece8da), tan tunic (#c0a070), brown strap, mottled brown trousers, yellow shoes (#d8b040), steel blade.
- Tinted (civ color): short sleeves/shoulders, waist sash, and vertical stripes/patches running down the trousers.
- Versus old remake: catalog says trousers are untinted with tan stripes; in the original the trouser stripes and the waist sash are blue. The tiara is a soft cloth hood with face veil.
