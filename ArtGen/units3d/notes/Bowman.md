# Bowman
- Pose: upright, feet apart; bow held in the left hand low in front at waist height, tilted roughly horizontal; right hand holds a loose arrow pointing down-forward.
- Gear: small round cap with a gold knob on top; black hair and beard; brown leather vest/cuirass with straps over a knee-length tunic; quiver of arrows on the back; brown leg wraps to the knee, sandals.
- Colors: dark brown leather (#6a4a28), tan wraps, gold knob (#e0b840), tan wood bow, pale arrow shafts.
- Tinted (civ color): the cap, the short sleeves/shoulders, and the knee-length tunic skirt.
- Versus old remake: catalog colors the cap brown, but in the original the cap is blue (tinted) with a gold knob; otherwise matches.
