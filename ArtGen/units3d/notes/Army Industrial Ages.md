- Pose: single officer standing feet apart, holding a tall flagpole vertically in front/at the right side with both hands at chest height; flag flies out to one side at top.
- Gear: black bicorne/cocked hat; knee-length frock coat with gold epaulettes and gold front buttons/trim; white waistcoat/shirt front; grey trousers; tall black boots.
- Colors: coat civ color, hat and boots near-black (~#1e1e22), trousers mid-grey (~#9a9ca2), epaulettes gold, white chest.
