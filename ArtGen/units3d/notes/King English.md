# King English
- Pose: female ruler (Elizabeth I) standing, the right hand holding a gold scepter diagonally across the chest; the other hand low at the waist.
- Gear: red hair in an updo with a small crown; large white lace ruff fanning up behind the head; tight bodice; puffed sleeves; very wide bell-shaped farthingale skirt with vertical gold stripes and jewel dots.
- Colors: gold stripes and trims (~#d8b030), ruff white, hair red (~#9a4a26), skin pale.
- Tinted (civ color): bodice, sleeves, and the skirt panels between the gold stripes, i.e. most of the dress.
- Versus old remake: the gown is mostly civ color, not brown-gold (#8e7e5a); the scepter has a plain gold head, not an orb; the ruff is a tall fan behind the head.
