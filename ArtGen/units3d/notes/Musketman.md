# Musketman
- Pose: upright, legs slightly apart; matchlock musket resting on the right shoulder, angled forward and upward; left hand holds a tall forked musket rest planted vertically on the ground.
- Gear: polished steel morion helmet with a feather plume, dark leather jerkin over the torso with a bandolier and a tan pouch at the hip, puffed breeches, grey stockings, dark shoes.
- Colors: steel grey helmet, dark grey-brown leather jerkin (#5a5048), grey stockings, brown musket stock, black rest pole.
- Tinted (civ color): the sleeves/arms, the breeches, and the helmet plume.
