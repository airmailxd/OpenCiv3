# WWII Paratrooper
- Pose: upright, feet apart; Thompson submachine gun held low across the hip, right hand on the grip, muzzle forward.
- Gear: netted M1 helmet, olive/khaki camo jump smock, US flag patch on the shoulder, harness, very large camo-wrapped pack/chute bundle on the back, banded trousers, brown boots.
- Colors: olive-khaki camo (#9e9562, #49432f, #2e2a20), olive helmet, brown boots, red/white flag patch.
- Tinted (civ color): the chest/vest front, a band at the waist, and the horizontal bands around the knees/shins.
- Versus old remake: knee bands should be tinted (catalog has them #4a4636) and the waist belt tinted; otherwise matches.
