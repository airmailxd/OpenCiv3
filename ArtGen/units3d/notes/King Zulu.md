# King Zulu
- Pose: ready stance; right hand holds a short wooden knobkerrie/club angled forward-down; left arm carries a tall oval cowhide shield upright at the side with a white feather tuft on top of its stick.
- Gear: tan feather/fur headdress fanned around the head; bare dark-skinned torso with fur shoulder cape; tan fur kilt to the knee; fur bands below the knees; armlets.
- Colors: dark brown skin (#5a3e2a), tan fur (#c4ac78), pale wood club, cream/white feathers, blue accents.
- Tinted (civ color): horizontal stripes on the shield, armlets/upper-arm bands, the shoulder-cape edge, bands on the kilt, and the below-knee bands.
- Versus old remake: weapon is a knobbed club, not an iklwa spear; tint should include arm/leg bands and kilt bands, not just the hide; no "point" gesture.
