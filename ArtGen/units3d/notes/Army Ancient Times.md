# Army Ancient Times
- Pose: single bearded elder standing upright, both hands gripping a tall wooden banner pole held vertically in front of the body; pole carries a horizontal crossbar from which a square flag hangs (vexillum style) well above the head.
- Gear: bare head, short grey-brown hair and beard; ankle-length coarse tan robe/cloak over a pale cream undergown visible down the front; sandals; flag has three vertical colored bars (red, green, cyan) on the field.
- Colors: robe tan/khaki (~#a08a6c), undergown off-white (~#dcd8cc), skin warm tan, pole and crossbar wood brown.
- Tinted (civ color): flag field, robe front lapel edges, sleeve cuffs, and two horizontal bands near the robe hem.
- Versus old remake: largely matches (bearded, robe, banner); catalog tint list omits the flag field and the double hem bands; flag must be a crossbar-hung square, not a side-attached flag.
