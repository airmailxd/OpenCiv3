# King Celtic
- Pose: standing relaxed, arms hanging at sides; no sword held or pointed.
- Gear: long dark hair; knee-length tartan tunic; a brown leather baldric diagonally across the chest; a long sword in a scabbard on the back with the gold hilt over one shoulder; gold shoulder clasp; leather boots/leg wraps.
- Colors: tunic civ color checked with dark green, leather brown (~#5a3a22), hilt gold, skin pale tan.
- Tinted (civ color): the tunic (as tartan with dark green checks).
- Versus old remake: catalog has the longsword held in the hand with a point gesture; in the original it is sheathed on the back.
