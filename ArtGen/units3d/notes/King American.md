# King American
- Pose: stately upright stance (Lincoln), arms hanging at sides with hands clasped behind the back (visible in rear views); no raised hand.
- Gear: tall black stovepipe top hat; chin beard; black knee-length frock coat; front waistcoat; white shirt collar; black trousers; brown shoes.
- Colors: almost all near-black (~#1e1c1c to #2a2826), white collar, brown shoes, skin tan.
- Tinted (civ color): the hat band and the waistcoat front panel down the chest.
- Versus old remake: gesture "raise left hand" is wrong; hands are lowered and clasped behind the back. Otherwise matches.
