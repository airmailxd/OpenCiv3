- Gear: gold crown/helmet with gold front disc; knee-length gold-and-brown checkerboard tunic; heavy shoulder-to-knee cape with a gold band and white zig-zag stepped pattern near the hem; gold leg bands and sandals; staff topped with a fan of long feathers.
