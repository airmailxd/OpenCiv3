# Hoplite
- Pose: upright and still; long spear held vertically in the right hand, butt on the ground, tip well above the head; large round shield carried on the left arm covering the front of the torso.
- Gear: bronze Corinthian helmet with tall front-to-back crest; bronze cuirass; brown leather pteruges skirt; bronze greaves; wristband on the spear arm.
- Colors: bronze/gold (#c8963e), shield face olive-gold with a dark painted emblem and red accent, brown wood shield back, dark brown spear.
- Tinted (civ color): helmet crest, shield rim, edge trim of the cuirass (shoulders, armholes, waist), and the wristband.
- Versus old remake: catalog misses the blue trim around the cuirass edges and the blue wristband.
