# Jaguar Warrior
- Pose: wide relaxed stance; right hand holds a short stone-headed club/macuahuitl low and angled out to the side; left forearm carries a round shield held at hip height facing forward.
- Gear: jaguar-skin hood/helmet with the jaguar face over the head and a tall feather spray rising from the crown; bare muscular torso with a gold collar/pectoral; gold armbands, wristbands, knee and ankle bands; spotted brown leg wraps; short loincloth.
- Colors: reddish-brown skin (#8a5634), tan-orange jaguar hide (#c87436) with dark spots, gold bands (#e0a838), gray-white stone club head on a wood haft.
- Tinted (civ color): head feather plume, a shoulder/collar band around the upper chest, waist belt and loincloth (front and back), wrist cuffs, and the round shield face (with a gold boss in the center).
- Versus old remake: catalog misses the tinted shoulder/collar band and wrist cuffs; shield face is civ color with a gold center boss, not a plain painted chimalli.
