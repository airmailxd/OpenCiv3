# King Indian
- Pose: slightly stooped, walking; left hand holds a tall thin walking stick planted vertically; right arm hangs.
- Gear: bald head; white shawl over the shoulders hanging long down the back; white dhoti wrapped to the knees; narrow sash diagonal from left shoulder to right hip; bare legs, sandals.
- Colors: white/light grey cloth (#e4e2dc), brown skin (#8e6a50), brown stick (#5a3a22).
- Tinted (civ color): the diagonal sash and the small tip at the top of the stick.
- Versus old remake: no raised right arm; long shawl down the back missing; otherwise matches.
