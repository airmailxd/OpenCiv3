# King German
- Pose: upright, feet apart; left fist on hip (elbow out); right arm hangs straight at side. No pointing.
- Gear: silver pickelhaube with tall spike; knee-length pale blue-grey military tunic/frock coat; large gold epaulettes; wide diagonal sash from left shoulder to right hip; white belt at waist; gold cuffs; black trousers and tall black boots.
- Colors: blue-grey coat (#8a9cb0), silver helmet (#a8acb4), gold epaulettes and cuffs (#e0c040), white belt, black legs (#18181a).
- Tinted (civ color): only the diagonal shoulder sash (plus a few specks on the collar/helmet front).
