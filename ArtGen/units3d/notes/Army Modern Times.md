# Army Modern Times
- Pose: single soldier standing, holding a tall flagpole vertically at his side/front with both hands; large flag flies out at top.
- Gear: round olive steel helmet (not a cap); olive-green field tunic; khaki/tan trousers; brown knee boots or puttees; a broad sash across the chest and shoulders edged in gold.
- Colors: tunic olive (~#5a6040), helmet dark olive, trousers khaki (~#a89a78), boots brown, sash gold-edged.
- Tinted (civ color): the chest sash/yoke and the flag field; flag bears a Union-Jack-like diagonal cross pattern in red, green and cyan.
- Versus old remake: headgear should be a round helmet, not a peaked cap; otherwise matches; add flag field to tint.
