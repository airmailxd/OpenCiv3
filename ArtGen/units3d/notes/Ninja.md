# Ninja
- Pose: loose, slightly crouched stance with legs apart; a kama (sickle) in each hand, both held low at the sides with blades curving outward and down.
- Gear: dark hood and face mask; long-sleeved dark jacket reaching mid-thigh with split skirt panels; dark baggy trousers; dark tabi boots; a harness of straps over the chest and back plus a belt.
- Colors: very dark olive/black-green cloth (#2a3020), tan wooden kama handles, pale steel blades.
- Tinted (civ color): the strap harness: straps over both shoulders forming a V/Y on the chest and back, joined to a waist belt.
- Versus old remake: catalog tints only the belt; the original also tints the shoulder straps crossing chest and back.
