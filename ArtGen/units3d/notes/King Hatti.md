# King Hatti
- Pose: upright; right hand holds a short gold scepter/mace low at hip level, horizontal and pointing forward; left arm hangs at side.
- Gear: gold circlet crown on dark hair; full dark beard; ankle-length cream robe with a dark vertical stitched seam line; short shawl/mantle wrapped over shoulders and chest; maroon apron panel at the front of the waist; gold belt band; band at the robe hem; bare feet/sandals.
- Colors: cream robe (#d8ccaa), maroon panel (#7a3226), gold circlet and scepter (#e0c030), dark brown hair/beard.
- Tinted (civ color): the shoulder mantle, the waist band, and the robe hem band.
