# King Byzantines
- Pose: female ruler (Theodora) standing upright, both hands held together at chest level holding a small white object.
- Gear: domed jeweled crown (gold with civ-color panels and a small top ornament); long dark hair down the back; floor-length white gown with a vertical front panel; full-length cape with a broad gold jeweled collar and gold hem border.
- Colors: gown white-cream (~#ece6d0), gold collar and trims, hair dark brown.
- Tinted (civ color): the cape, the gown front panel and sleeves, and the crown panels.
- Versus old remake: matches overall; add sleeves and front panel to the tint list; cape is floor-length with a broad gold hem band.
