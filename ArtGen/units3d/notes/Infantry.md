# Infantry
- Pose: square stance, legs apart; long rifle (Garand-like, thin bayonet) held horizontal across the waist with both hands, muzzle forward, right hand at the stock.
- Gear: grey steel M1-style helmet, field shirt, large blocky field pack high on the back, webbing, mottled camo trousers, light grey/white gaiters over the shins, brown boots.
- Colors: steel grey helmet (#6c6c66), brown/tan mottled trousers (#8a7a5e/#a4967c), off-white gaiters, brown shoes, dark grey rifle.
- Tinted (civ color): the shirt/upper torso AND the big back pack (pack is solid blue from behind).
