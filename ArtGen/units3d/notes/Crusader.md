# Crusader
- Pose: upright; sword in the right hand held low, blade angled down-forward; heater/kite shield on the left arm held in front of the body.
- Gear: closed grey great helm (flat-top, dark eye slit), knee-length surcoat over mail, grey mail sleeves and leggings, belt, gold spurs/ankle details.
- Colors: off-white surcoat (#e4e0d6), steel grey mail and helm, tan wood/leather shield back and rim, small gold accents at the feet.
- Tinted (civ color): the surcoat front carries a white cross on blue quarters, with blue shoulders and a blue hem band; the shield face is blue with a white cross.
- Versus old remake: shield is blue with a white cross and heater/kite shaped (catalog has an oval, white face with a tinted emblem); the helmet is a closed great helm, not a cap; surcoat hem is tinted too.
