# Legionary
- Pose: square stance; right hand holds a gladius low, blade pointing down and slightly forward; left arm holds a tall curved scutum upright in front of the left side of the body.
- Gear: steel galea helmet with a short upright crest; steel segmented plate on the torso with pale shoulder plates; a cape draped over both shoulders and down the back to the waist; dark red tunic skirt; dark greaves/leg wraps; bare tan legs and sandals.
- Colors: steel gray (#a8a8b0), dark red tunic (#7a2622), tan skin (#d0985c), shield face medium gray with a darker rim.
- Tinted (civ color): helmet crest, the shoulder cape (covers the whole upper back and shoulders), the hem band below the tunic skirt, and the winged/thunderbolt emblem on the shield face (the shield field itself is gray).
- Versus old remake: catalog misses the large tinted cape over shoulders and back and the tinted skirt hem; it tints the whole shield face, but only the emblem is civ color on a gray shield.
