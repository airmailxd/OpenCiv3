# Javelin Thrower
- Pose: upright stance; left hand grips a tall bundle of 3-4 javelins planted vertically on the ground beside him (taller than the figure); right hand holds a single javelin low and horizontal, point forward.
- Gear: close-fitting head cover/hood with yellow feather tufts on top and a dark red face/mask area; sleeveless top covering chest and back; short loincloth flap; bands at upper arm, wrist, knee and ankle; yellow feather tassels tied on the javelin shafts; bare feet/lower legs.
- Colors: brown skin (#8a5a3a), yellow-gold feathers and wrist tassels (#e8b830), dark red face paint/mask, brown wooden shafts.
- Tinted (civ color): the head cover, the whole sleeveless torso top (front and back), loincloth, arm/knee/ankle bands, and small wraps on each javelin shaft.
- Versus old remake: catalog has top 'bare' but the original wears a tinted sleeveless top over the torso; javelin bundle is planted upright and very tall, not a small carried bundle; headdress is a hood with small yellow tufts, not a big feather crown; shaft wraps are also tinted.
