# Pikeman
- Pose: upright stance; right hand holds a pike vertically, butt on the ground beside the right foot, head about one-third of a body length above the helmet; left arm relaxed at the side.
- Gear: steel morion/kettle helmet with brim; steel breastplate and back plate; steel tassets over the hips; puffy sleeves; dark short breeches; pale cream hose/stockings; brown shoes. Pike head has small side lugs (partisan-like).
- Colors: steel gray (#a8a8b0), dark navy breeches, cream stockings, brown shoes and wooden shaft, light beard.
- Tinted (civ color): both sleeves, a cross/emblem on the breastplate, and the breeches below the tassets (dark-shaded civ color).
- Versus old remake: catalog gives gray trousers, but the original has dark tinted breeches over cream stockings; pike is shorter than the catalog's 2.9 length (roughly 1.3x figure height).
