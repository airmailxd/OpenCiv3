# Shogun
- Pose: two-handed katana held raised at shoulder level, blade angled up and back over the right shoulder, ready to strike.
- Gear: kabuto helmet with gold crest; dark grey kataginu shoulder vest; ankle-length wide-sleeved robe with a dark central front panel; white V collar; gold bands at hem and cuffs; white tabi socks/sandals.
- Colors: blue robe and helmet, dark grey vest/panel (#3e3c40), gold trim (#d8b030), white collar, silver blade.
- Tinted (civ color): the robe (body and sleeves) and the helmet bowl.
- Versus old remake: katana is held two-handed and raised, not one-handed with a pointing left hand; helmet is tinted, not dark brown; robe reaches the ankles.
