# King Egyptian
- Pose: walking stance, one hand on the hip; the other hand holds a long gold crook/whip-like staff with curled hook ends, held low and angled out to the side.
- Gear: striped nemes headdress with a small gold uraeus on top; gold broad collar; white pleated ankle-length kilt with a hanging front panel; gold arm bands; sandals.
- Colors: kilt white (~#f0ece0), gold accents, skin dark brown (~#8a5a30).
- Tinted (civ color): nemes stripes, the front apron panel, and bands on the staff.
- Versus old remake: the held item is a long curled crook/staff, not a short flail; the arm is low, not raised.
