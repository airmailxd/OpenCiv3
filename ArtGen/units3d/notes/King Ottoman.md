# King Ottoman
- Pose: upright, stately; both hands together in front at the waist.
- Gear: large round white turban with a front aigrette (plume and jewel); dark beard; ankle-length white kaftan with long sleeves; sleeveless over-vest to the waist with gold edging; gold belt; hem trim and side slits; brown shoes.
- Colors: white/light grey (#f0ece2), gold trim (#e0c030), dark beard, brown shoes (#6a4a2a).
- Tinted (civ color): the waist-length vest, the kaftan hem and slit edges, and part of the turban plume.
- Versus old remake: vest stops at the waist with a gold belt, not hip length; hem trim also tinted; shoes are brown, not gold.
