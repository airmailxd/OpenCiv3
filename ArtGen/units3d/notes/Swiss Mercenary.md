# Swiss Mercenary
- Pose: upright stance; right hand holds a long pike vertically, butt on the ground, head well above the helmet; left arm relaxed.
- Gear: steel morion helmet with a feather plume; polished steel breastplate; a broad sash running diagonally from one shoulder across the chest to the opposite hip; puffy slashed sleeves; baggy red breeches slashed with black stripes; black shoes; brown beard.
- Colors: bright steel (#c0c0c8), red breeches (#9a3428) with black stripes, pale sleeves, black shoes.
- Tinted (civ color): helmet plume, the diagonal chest sash, and most of the pike shaft (steel head and butt untinted).
- Versus old remake: catalog tints 'top' (tunic) and colors the chest strap brown; in the original the chest sash is the civ color and there is no tinted tunic.
