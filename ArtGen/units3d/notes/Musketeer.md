# Musketeer
- Pose: upright; musket held VERTICAL in front of the body with both hands (right low on the stock, left near chest), muzzle straight up; the muzzle is flared/bell-shaped, pale steel.
- Gear: wide-brimmed cavalier hat with a large feather plume, knee-length coat with skirt, diagonal baldric sash over the left shoulder, dark breeches, tall black boots.
- Colors: maroon/dusty red coat and hat (#7d4742 / #4a2a2b), dark brown-black breeches and boots, pale steel muzzle, brown stock.
- Tinted (civ color): the hat plume, the diagonal baldric sash across chest/back, a wide band at the coat hem/waist, and the boot-top/knee bands.
- Versus old remake: tint should be plume + baldric + coat hem + knee bands, not a jerkin vest; hold is vertical in front, not port-arms diagonal.
