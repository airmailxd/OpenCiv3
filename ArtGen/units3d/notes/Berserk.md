# Berserk
- Pose: brawny, wide-legged slight crouch; long-handled battle axe held two-handed, low and roughly horizontal across the hips, big pale-steel axe head out to his right side at knee height.
- Gear: small conical iron helmet; long bright-yellow braids/beard hanging down the chest; huge shaggy brown fur pelt over shoulders and the whole back down to the thighs; long-sleeved tunic; baggy trousers; brown fur-wrapped boots to the knee.
- Colors: brown fur (#6a4a30), yellow braids (#d8c020), grey helmet, pale steel axe head (#d0d0d0), tan haft, ruddy skin.
- Tinted (civ color): tunic torso and sleeves, wristbands, and trousers.
- Versus old remake: catalog tints only the trousers; the tunic/sleeves and wristbands are also blue. Fur is a full back-covering cloak, not a vest.
