# King Greek
- Pose: upright, calm; both forearms bent forward with hands meeting at the waist in front. No raised arm.
- Gear: short dark curly hair with a headband; sleeveless white chiton to ankles; large himation draped over the left shoulder, wrapping across the back and around the hips/thighs in heavy folds to the knees and below; gold necklace/collar at the neck; gold sandals.
- Colors: white/pale grey chiton (#e4e0d6), tan skin, dark brown hair, gold collar and sandals (#e0b830).
- Tinted (civ color): the whole himation drape and the headband.
