# Chasquis Scout
- Pose: relaxed upright stance, arms slightly out; small star-headed mace (dark stone head, short wooden handle) held in the right hand low, angled down and forward.
- Gear: large fan/halo headdress framing the face; long black hair down the back; bare muscular torso; short wrap skirt with gold belt and gold hem; bands at wrists, below knees, and ankles; barefoot.
- Colors: brown skin (#a86c44), black hair, gold trim (#e0b030), white/gold edging on the headdress, dark grey mace head.
- Tinted (civ color): headdress fan, skirt, and the wrist, knee and ankle bands.
- Versus old remake: matches well; the headdress is a broad rounded fan surrounding the whole head (blue with white/gold rim), not small feathers.
