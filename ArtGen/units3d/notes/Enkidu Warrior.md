# Enkidu Warrior
- Pose: upright, feet apart; long wooden spear held vertically in one hand at the side, butt on the ground, tip above the head; free arm hangs slightly out.
- Gear: long black hair and long black chest-length beard; headband with ribbon streamers flicking up off the top of the head; shaggy light-tan fur wrapping the torso and a fur cape down the back to the calves; fur kilt; bands on upper arms, wrists, waist and knees; barefoot with pale ankle wraps.
- Colors: light tan/beige mottled fur (#b09a70), black hair/beard, tan skin, dark red-brown spear (#6a3e24).
- Tinted (civ color): headband and streamers, upper-arm bands, wristbands, waist belt band, knee bands.
- Versus old remake: torso is covered in fur, not bare; fur is much lighter tan than the catalog pelt (#6e5a44); the kilt itself is fur, only the waist and knee bands are blue; streamers are also blue.
