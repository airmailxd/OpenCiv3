# King Spanish
- Pose: standing tall and slender; left hand on hip (elbow out); right hand holds a gold scepter low, angled down and forward at hip height.
- Gear: tall gold open crown; blue hood/wimple framing the face; long white veil-cape from shoulders to floor, open at front; fitted blue gown with long blue sleeves, scattered gold fleur-de-lis; wide flared hem spilling on the ground with white/gold lace and blue edge.
- Colors: blue gown, white veil (#e8e8e8), gold crown/fleurs/scepter (#d8b030), pale skin.
- Tinted (civ color): bodice, sleeves, skirt (under gold fleurs), the hood around the face, and the outer edge of the hem spread on the ground.
- Versus old remake: "present" gesture is wrong - left hand is on the hip and the scepter is held low in the right; gown pattern is fleur-de-lis not stripes; hood is tinted and hair is not visible.
