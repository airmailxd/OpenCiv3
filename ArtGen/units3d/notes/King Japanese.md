# King Japanese
- Pose: upright, wide and stately; both fists on hips with elbows out.
- Gear: tall court cap with an upright tail on top; wide-shouldered ankle-length robe with broad sleeves; cream/white V collar; dark grey-black front panel down the middle with gold edges; white belt; gold bands at the cuffs and hem; pale shoes.
- Colors: gold trim (#d8b030), dark grey panel (#3a3a40), cream collar (#ece8e0), black hair.
- Tinted (civ color): almost the entire outer robe and sleeves, plus the cap.
- Versus old remake: the robe body down to the ankles is tinted, not just top/kataginu; grey hakama only shows as the narrow front panel; cap is tinted, not black; gesture is both hands on hips, not pointing.
