# King Portugal
- Pose: upright; right hand holds a gold-hilted short sword low and forward at hip; left forearm raised to the chest/hat brim.
- Gear: wide round-brimmed dark brown hat with gold edge; long dark hair and grey beard; hip-length doublet with gold buttons, trim and puffed shoulders; white shirt front; grey hose with gold garters; black shoes.
- Colors: gold trim (#e0c030), dark brown hat (#4a3020), grey hose (#a0a0a8), black shoes.
- Tinted (civ color): the whole doublet body and sleeves (gold trim stays gold).
- Versus old remake: the doublet itself is tinted, not dark brown with tinted stripes; hat is a broad-brimmed hat, not a chaperon; sword is short and low; left arm is raised to the chest, not pointing.
