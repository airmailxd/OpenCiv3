# King Viking
- Pose: standing square; right hand holds a long sword point-down and back, angled low behind the leg; left arm at side under the cape.
- Gear: round helmet with gold rim and gold nose/crest strip; long reddish-blond hair and beard; grey mail/padded tunic with gold belt and gold collar/chest bands; brown baggy trousers; grey-brown wrapped boots; full-length cape from shoulders to ankles.
- Colors: grey mail (#7e8288), gold trim (#d8b030), brown trousers (#5a4630), orange-blond hair (#c08040), blue cape and helmet.
- Tinted (civ color): the full cape and the helmet bowl (gold trim stays gold).
- Versus old remake: helmet is tinted blue in the original, catalog keeps it grey; cape reaches the ankles (catalog hem is fine); sword is held low point-down rather than a raised gesture with the left.
