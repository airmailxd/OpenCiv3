# Impi
- Pose: crouched, bow-legged ready stance; short broad-bladed stabbing spear in the right hand, low and angled forward-down; tall oval cowhide shield on the left arm, held upright beside the body.
- Gear: bushy feather headdress; bare dark torso with brown hide straps/ornaments; brown hide loincloth; arm and wrist bands; big furry cow-tail tufts around the calves with yellow bands above.
- Colors: very dark brown skin (#5e3c26), black-and-white mottled shield with a dark stick behind, brown hide, yellow band accents, pale steel spear blade.
- Tinted (civ color): headdress, arm/wrist bands, and the leg tufts.
- Versus old remake: matches.
