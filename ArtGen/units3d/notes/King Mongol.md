# King Mongol
- Pose: upright; right hand holds a curved scimitar low beside the thigh, blade angled down/out; left arm hangs loosely.
- Gear: grey fur-brimmed hat with a pointed top; dark beard; long ochre deel to mid-shin, flared skirt; chest panel/vest with gold edge trim; brown boots.
- Colors: ochre-gold deel (#c09040), grey fur (#8a8a88), silver blade, gold hilt (#e0c030), brown boots.
- Tinted (civ color): the hat crown and the chest panel/vest front and back.
- Versus old remake: no pointing; blade held low; tint is on the chest panel, not the hem band or sash; fur is grey, not dark brown.
