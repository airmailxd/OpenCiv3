# Scout
- Pose: relaxed, slightly hunched walking stance; right hand holds a small brown gourd/pouch low at the side; left arm hangs loose.
- Gear: bald head with a long dark ponytail down the back; beard; bare muscular torso with a thin brown strap and small pack across the chest; wide upper-arm bands; short loincloth flap front and back; baggy tan leggings; brown boots.
- Colors: tan-brown skin (#b07a52), dark brown hair (#2a1e14), tan leggings (#c0a878), brown leather boots and strap.
- Tinted (civ color): the wide upper-arm bands on both arms and the loincloth flaps (front and back).
- Versus old remake: catalog also tints the chest strap, but in the original the strap is plain brown leather.
