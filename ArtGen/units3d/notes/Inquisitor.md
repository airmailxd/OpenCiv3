# Inquisitor
- Pose: upright and solemn; open book raised in the left hand at chest-to-head height as if reading; short torch in the right hand hanging low, angled down, small orange-red flame.
- Gear: floor-length dark grey hooded robe, hood up hiding the face; gold cross on the chest; gold sash at the waist; wide sleeves; sandals barely visible.
- Colors: charcoal grey robe (#46464a), gold cross and sash (#d0b040), white book pages, brown torch with orange flame.
- Tinted (civ color): book cover, a band/collar over the shoulders and chest, and the sleeve cuffs.
- Versus old remake: catalog tints the belt, but the belt/sash is gold in the original; the shoulder band and sleeve cuffs are blue. Book is held higher (chest to head height) than the catalog stance.
