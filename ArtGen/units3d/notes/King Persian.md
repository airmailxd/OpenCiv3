# King Persian
- Pose: upright; arms hanging at the sides, slightly away from the body. No raised arm.
- Gear: spiked gold crown; long wavy brown hair and beard; short-sleeved white tunic to mid-thigh with a diagonal patterned band across the chest and a patterned hem; gold wrist bracers; bands wrapped on the lower legs; brown boots.
- Colors: white tunic (#e8e4da), gold crown and bracers (#e0c030), brown hair and legs (#6e5a46).
- Tinted (civ color): the patterned chest decoration, the tunic hem band, and the leg bands.
- Versus old remake: no raised right arm, arms are down; leg wraps are tinted; otherwise matches.
