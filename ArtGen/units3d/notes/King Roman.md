# King Roman
- Pose: upright, relaxed; right hand holds a tall gold eagle-topped staff vertically at the side, butt near the ground; left arm hangs low under the draped cape. No raised arm.
- Gear: bare head with short brown hair; gold muscle cuirass with blue patches at chest and shoulders; short white tunic with grey-white pteruges/skirt strips; brown laced boots to mid-shin; long cape over the left shoulder, wrapping the left arm and hanging to the calves at back.
- Colors: gold cuirass and staff (#d8b040), white/light grey tunic (#e8e8e4), tan skin, brown boots (#6a4a2a).
- Tinted (civ color): the whole cape (front drape over left arm and full back), plus blue panels/straps on the upper cuirass and shoulders.
