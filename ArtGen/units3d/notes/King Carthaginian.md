# King Carthaginian
- Pose: standing feet apart, arms relaxed at sides, a sheathed short sword/dagger at the hip; no pointing gesture.
- Gear: bald head; grey scale/mail cuirass with a scaled back cape; gold shoulder pieces; brown baggy trousers gathered into brown boots; wristbands.
- Colors: mail steel grey (~#7a8088), trousers brown (~#5a4a3a), pauldrons gold, skin tan.
- Tinted (civ color): the wristbands and a vertical stripe down the side of each trouser leg.
- Versus old remake: "point" gesture is wrong; tinted belt is wrong, the trouser side stripes are tinted instead.
