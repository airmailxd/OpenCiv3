# Artillery (realism pass, siege batch)

**Model.** A WWII towed field gun (75 mm class): a long barrel with a muzzle brake and recoil cylinders, a split
gun shield, two pressed-steel wheels with fat tyres, the split trail opened behind with spades; one gunner in M1
helmet and olive drab at the breech.

**Must read at 1x:** the long barrel with the brake; the shield; the opened split trail; the wheels.

**Civ colour:** the shield's top edges and face panels, the trail spades. Stock 7.3 %
(5.4/5.8/5.2/4.2/11.9/13.1/8.5/4.3).
