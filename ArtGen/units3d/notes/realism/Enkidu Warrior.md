# Enkidu Warrior: realism design (2026-11-23)

## The real-world model

A Sumerian warrior of the Early Dynastic period, about 2600-2400 BC, with the look Civ3 gives Gilgamesh's wild
companion Enkidu: the Standard of Ur and the Stele of the Vultures show the period's spearmen in fleece kilts
(kaunakes), long spears and heavy beards.

- a **kaunakes**: a skirt and cloak of tufted fleece or fur;
- a **long spear** of about 2.5 m with a leaf blade of copper;
- long hair and a long beard; a headband;
- bare feet.

Civ3's original: long black hair and a chest-length beard, a civ headband with streamers, shaggy light-tan fur
over the torso and down the back, a fur kilt, civ bands on the upper arms, wrists, waist and knees; the long
spear upright at his side.

## The features that must read at 1x

1. The **long spear held ready**, tipped forward, the copper blade well above the head.
2. The **pale shaggy fur** over the torso and back: a light figure among dark ones.
3. The **black hair and long beard** under the civ headband.
4. The **civ bands** at arms, wrists, waist and knees.

## The pose

Weight on the right leg; the right fist at chest height on the spear, tipped forward; the left hand free and
open at the side; the torso upright.

## Civ colour

Stock: the shipped Enkidu Warrior 6-13 % (mean 10): low. Aim 15-30 % in every direction. Regions: the
**headband**, the **arm, wrist, waist and knee bands** (wider).

## Stock neighbours

Spearman and Warrior.

## Rounds

### Build rounds 1-3
- The spear raised ready; the civ bands widened twice (7-13 % -> 8-16 %, then upper arm 0.1, waist 0.15, knee 0.1).

### Critic round 1 (batch 3): 60.5 / 59.5 = 60
Both: the fleece one flat speckled sack, no tiered tufts; the civ colour only thin bands (12 % mean, 8-9 % from
behind). One: the white anklets read as footwear; the hair a black blob from behind. Fixes: no anklets, a civ baldric.

### Critic round 2 (batch 3): 62 / 57 = 59.5
The girdle did not show (the fleece covers the waist) and the baldric hid under it: civ 8-17 % (mean 12.5), the
fresh critic calls it critical from behind. Both: the kaunakes a flat sack. Fixes: a civ wool skirt below the fleece
kilt ('bottom': 'skirt', tinted), a copper blade.

### Critic round 3 (batch 3): 64.5 / 61.5 = 63
Landed: the civ skirt is a mass of colour from behind; the copper blade. Still: the kaunakes one flat beige sack;
from the front the skirt hides and the colour is bands only.

## Final: round 3, 63 (the cap)
Civ 10-19 % (mean 14). Open points: the kaunakes as 3-4 tiers of pointed tufts with the right shoulder bare (needs a
new garment: the pelt shell is one noise texture); the civ skirt longer in front; the hair in locks from behind.

Animations re-rendered from the final still (build.py --anim, 2026-11-23) and checked at 2x (directions 1, 3, 6): ATTACK1, FORTIFY, RUN and DEATH play cleanly; in direction 1 the thrust or cut points at the camera, so it reads foreshortened.
