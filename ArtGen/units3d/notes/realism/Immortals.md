# Immortals: realism design (2026-11-23)

## The real-world model

The Persian Immortals (the Anusiya) of the Achaemenid army, about 500-470 BC, as on the glazed-brick frieze of
the archers at Susa and in Herodotus (VII.83):

- a soft cloth **tiara** or headcloth, its lappets wrapped over the chin and mouth;
- a long-sleeved tunic and **trousers** (anaxyrides), embroidered, a belt, soft yellow shoes;
- in the field a **spear** with a silver or gold pomegranate butt, a bow and a quiver; Civ3 arms them with a
  curved sword (the akinakes is short and straight; the scimitar is Civ's reading); a wicker shield.

Civ3's original: a white headcloth with a face veil, a tan tunic with civ short sleeves, a brown baldric, a civ
waist sash, baggy trousers with civ stripes, yellow shoes, gold bracelets, a curved sword low and swept out.

## The features that must read at 1x

1. The **white headcloth and veil**: a pale head over a dark-skinned face.
2. The **curved sword held ready**: out at his right side at the hip, the blade forward and up, bright, a dark
   grip; never hanging at the thigh.
3. The **civ sash and sleeves** on a tan tunic, the striped trousers.
4. The **yellow shoes** and gold bracelets.

## The pose

Weight on the right leg, the left foot forward; the left fist forward at the belt (a guard), the right fist at
the hip with the blade forward-out and up; the torso upright (the shipped forward lean goes).

## Civ colour

Stock: the shipped Immortals 31-41 % (mean 36): too much. Aim 15-30 % in every direction. Regions: the **short
sleeves**, the **sash**, and **narrow stripes** down the trousers.

## Stock neighbours

Swordsman and Archer.

## Rounds

### Build rounds 1-2
- Upright (the shipped forward lean gone), the sabre ready at the hip; the civ trouser stripes made dark (36 % ->
  13 %), then a broad civ sash: 12-16 %.

### Critic round 1 (batch 2): 65.5 / 63.5 = 64.5
Both: the sabre a white hairline at 1x; balloon shoulders; flat cloth; the face veil missing (a dark beard showed);
a passive off hand. Fixes: a wider sabre (0.075) in darker steel, the beard off so the veil shows, slimmer arms
(upper bulk 1.06), a quiver on the back (they carried bows).

### Critic round 2 (batch 2): 69 / 67 = 68
Landed: slimmer shoulders, the sabre, the quiver; the veil partly. Still: a slack pose with the blade trailing at the
knee; the veil below the mouth. Fixes: the hilt at the hip and the blade forward and up; the veil up to the nose.

### Critic round 3 (batch 2): 71.5 / 74 = 72.75

## Final (round 3)
Scores: R1 65.5 / 63.5 = 64.5; R2 69 / 67 = 68; **R3 71.5 / 74 = 72.75, final** (cap). No critical failure. Civ
10-14 % (mean 12: below the 15 % aim; the civ trouser stripes put it at 36 %, a middle setting is open). Open points:
in directions 0-2 the blade still points down and back; the civ shoulders bulbous ('puffy' layer) and plain, they
want folds; the veil reads as a flat mask.
