# Flak (realism pass, siege batch)

**Model.** A WWII quadruple 20 mm anti-aircraft gun (Flakvierling 38 type) on its two-wheeled trailer: cruciform
platform with levelling jacks, four barrels in a 2x2 block raised high, box magazines, a curved shield, a lattice
drawbar; a seated gunner and a standing loader in olive drab.

**Must read at 1x:** the four raised barrels; the curved shield; the trailer wheels and outriggers; the crew.

**Civ colour:** the shield's edges, the wheel hubs, the mudguard patches, the seat back, the crew's arm bands.
Stock 5.8 % (5.1/4.0/5.2/6.2/6.6/6.5/7.2/5.8).
