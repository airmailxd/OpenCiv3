# Hwacha (realism pass, siege batch)

**Model.** A Joseon Korean hwacha (15th-16th c.): a two-wheeled handcart with long shafts, a slatted bed and a
tilted launcher box packed with rows of singijeon fire arrows (red powder tubes, iron heads, white fletching);
two crewmen in blue-grey robes and straw hats, one with a lit fuse torch.

**Must read at 1x:** the arrow rack's grid of shafts; the cart wheels and shafts; the two robed crew with hats.

**Civ colour:** the shafts, the bed's rails (striped with timber fillers, as the original), the launcher frame
posts and panels, the crew's sashes. Stock 13.6 % (16.8/17.1/14.6/11.5/12.0/11.9/10.6/14.1).
Stock specks: dirs 5/6/7 (single-pixel arrow tips).

## Build rounds
- b1: warmer timber (two tones); thicker shafts (0.02) and fletching: specks down to 3-4 single pixels.
- b2: blunt, shorter arrowheads (0.026/0.07/0.012), shafts 0.022: clean in every direction.
- b3: the bed all timber (no civ stripes): 5.0 %, too far under the stock: kept the stripes.
Chosen (siege.REALISM['Hwacha']): shaft 0.022, fletch (0.006, 0.18, 0.07), head (0.026, 0.07, 0.012), warm timber.
