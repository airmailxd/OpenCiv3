# Ninja: realism design (2026-11-23)

## The real-world model

A shinobi of the Sengoku period (about 1550-1600; the Iga and Koga men of the Bansenshukai, 1676). The
all-black suit is a stage convention; the real men wore dark indigo or brown-grey work clothes. Here a deep
indigo-charcoal suit with value steps, so the figure reads in parts and not as a black blob:

- a **hood and mask** (zukin) of the darkest cloth, the eyes' slot showing skin;
- a **jacket** (uwagi) a step lighter, narrow sleeves, its skirt to the upper thigh;
- **trousers** tied below the knee, then **leg wraps** (kyahan) a step lighter again, and **tabi**;
- a **straight short sword** (ninjato, about 55 cm), bright-edged, with a dark square-guarded grip; its black
  scabbard slung across the back.

Civ3's original: all in near-black, hood and mask, civ straps crossing the chest and a civ belt, a blade in
each hand.

## The features that must read at 1x

1. The **blade**: bright, about 2 px wide, held ready in the right hand forward and out, clear of the dark
   body in all 8 directions.
2. The **hooded, masked head** with the skin of the eyes.
3. The **dark suit in steps of value**: hood darkest, jacket mid, wraps lighter, with the civ straps.
4. The **scabbard across the back** (a long dark diagonal) from behind.

## The pose

The torso upright (no crouch-hunch), the legs a half step apart and the knees soft, weight on the rear leg.
The right fist forward at the chest, the blade angled up and out; the left hand forward at the belly with a
kama, a guard.

## Civ colour

Stock: the shipped Ninja 9-15 % (mean 12); foot range 9-34. Aim about 12-20 %: the **crossed chest straps**
(the harness), the **belt (obi)**.

## Stock neighbours

Samurai and Swordsman.

## Rounds

### Build rounds 1-2
- R1: an indigo-charcoal suit in value steps, a straight bright ninjato (yoroi.katana_drawn, short and wide) held
  ready, a kama in the left; the empty scabbard behind. units.equip now imports yoroi when a prop's builder lives
  there. R2: a longer, wider blade, a bigger kama. Civ 9-16 % (mean 12).

### Critic round 1 (batch civil 1): 71 / 69 = 70
Both: the suit flat mid-grey (a costume), the arms splayed wide, the kama lost at 1x; bare feet. Fixes: an
indigo-black suit, the fists in close and forward, a bigger kama, slimmer shoulders, tabi.

### Critic round 2 (batch civil 1): 79 / 76 = 77.5
Landed: the indigo-black suit, the tabi, the guard (no longer hulking). Still: the kama a thin hooked pole at 1x
(scale stretched its haft); a flat suit. Fix: the kama a forearm long with a bigger blade (props.kama 'blade', new).

### Critic round 3 (batch civil 1): 80 / 73 = 76.5
Landed: the kama's hook in 0-3. Still: the kama end-on in 6-7; one flat value on the suit (the jacket's crossed
front, a sleeve tie, folds); the fresh critic saw a near-black silhouette.

## Final (round 2)
Scores: R1 71 / 69 = 70; **R2 79 / 76 = 77.5, final**; R3 80 / 73 = 76.5 (the forearm kama, props.kama 'blade';
the entry is back on round 2's kama, length 0.46 scale 1.75; the re-render matches round 2's sheet to 1/255). Cap
of three batch rounds; no critical failure; not 85. Civ 11-18 % (mean 14). Open points: the kama end-on in 6-7 (a
forearm haft with a bigger blade read better at 1x but scored lower overall); one flat value on the suit (the
jacket's crossed front, a sleeve tie, fold shading); one critic still saw a near-black silhouette at 1x.

The melee templates (attack, fortify, run, death) from the final still, with his own sword and kama; checked at 2x.
