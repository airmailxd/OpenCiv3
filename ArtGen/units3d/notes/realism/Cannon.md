# Cannon (realism pass, siege batch)

**Model.** A 17th-18th century field gun: a bronze barrel with reinforce rings, cascabel and dolphins on a
two-cheeked carriage with a short trail, two big spoked wheels with iron tyres; the gunner (the Musketman's
plumed morion) with an upright rammer.

**Must read at 1x:** the long barrel past the wheels; the two big civ-spoked wheels; the gunner.

**Civ colour:** the wheel spokes, the trail cheeks, the gunner's sleeves and breeches.
Stock 26.5 % (29.9/18.6/27.0/28.9/29.5/17.2/29.3/31.2).

## Build rounds
- b1: bronze vs dark iron barrel, wooden spokes (17.8 %) vs civ spokes: the shipped chrome barrel and white tyres
  read as a toy; wooden spokes lost the Civ3 identity and the colour.
- b2: civ spokes at 1.2 (was 1.6: they merged into blue discs), dark iron tyres; muted bronze vs iron: bronze
  reads apart from every other gun at 1x and is the period's field-gun metal.
- b3: bronze darkened (#7a5c34): 24.8 %.
Chosen (siege.REALISM['Cannon']): spoke_scale 1.2, gun_iron #7a5c34, band #4a3820, rim #3e3c3a.
