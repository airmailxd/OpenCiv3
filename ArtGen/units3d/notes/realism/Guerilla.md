# Guerilla: realism design (PC2, realism/infantry)

## The real-world model
An irregular fighter: a head wrap, a scarf at the neck, an olive T-shirt, faded woodland trousers, boots, crossed leather bandoliers, a small pack; the AK.

## The features that must read at 1x
1. the AK with its curved magazine at the low ready; 2. the head wrap; 3. the bare forearms and T-shirt; 4. the crossed bandoliers.

## The pose
Relaxed, the AK at the hip across the body, the muzzle forward and out.

## Civ colour
Civ on the head wrap, the neck scarf and the armbands. The shipped unit is 3 % (the head band only). Target: 15-30 % in every direction (foot stock 9-34 %), in 2-3 painted regions.

## Stock neighbours
Infantry and Marine

## Rounds
### Build rounds 1-4
- R1: an olive T-shirt, faded woodland trousers, brown leather bandoliers, a civ head band, neck scarf and armbands; the AK at the low ready across the body (it was held upright in the fist): 8 %.
- R2: the face read as a black blob under the band (dark skin, full beard): no beard, a lighter skin.
- R3: the neck scarf narrower (at 0.12 it read as a cape); the AK whole (it broke into pieces at 1x): clean; 8-11 %, mean 10 %.

### Critic batch 1: 75 / 86 = 80.5 (A: face critical)

### Round 2 (fixes)
- Face lit (`face_fill` 5, a lighter skin); matte trousers, not puffed (the rim-light halo); the AK angled out past the right hip, so it shows from behind; a narrower neck scarf; flat civ armbands. Civ 7-10 %, mean 8 %.

### Critic batch 2: 77 / 86 = 81.5

### Round 3 (the last; fixes)
- A shorter inseam (macro proportions 0.6, height 0.72); a slightly larger civ yoke (0.08). The rim halo along the trousers: matte cloth already; not removed further. Civ 7-11 %, mean 9.5 %.

### Critic batch 3: 79 / 86 = 82.5

## FINAL: R3 82.5
