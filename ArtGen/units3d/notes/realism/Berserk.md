# Berserk: realism design (2026-11-23)

## The real-world model

A Norse warrior of the Viking Age, about 850-1050 (the berserkir of the sagas and of Haraldskvæði; the
Gjermundbu helmet, the Mammen axe, the Lewis chessmen's shield-biters):

- a **Dane axe**: a broad thin bearded head of about 25 cm on a haft of 1.1-1.5 m, swung in both hands;
- a **spangenhelm or plain conical helmet** of iron (no horns);
- a wool **tunic** with long sleeves and **trousers**, leg wraps, turn-shoes;
- a **bear or wolf pelt** over the shoulders (the "bear-shirt" that gave the name);
- long hair and beard, sometimes braided.

Civ3's original: brawny, a wide crouch, the long axe two-handed low across the hips with the big pale head out at
his right; long yellow braids and beard, a small conical iron helmet, a huge shaggy brown fur down the back to
the thighs, a civ tunic with sleeves, civ wristbands and trousers.

## The features that must read at 1x

1. The **long axe in both hands, raised**: the haft diagonal across the front, the pale broad head up by his
   right shoulder (the shipped low-across hold hides the head against the legs in half the directions).
2. The **shaggy fur cloak** over the shoulders and back.
3. The **conical iron helmet and yellow braids/beard**.
4. The **civ tunic and trousers**.

## The pose

Feet wide, weight on the right leg, the knees soft; the torso upright; the right fist high at the shoulder near
the head, the left fist low at the left hip on the haft's end: the axe diagonal, head up and out at his right.

## Civ colour

Stock: the shipped Berserk 10-24 % (mean 18). Aim 15-30 % in every direction. Regions: the **tunic sleeves**,
the **trousers** and the **wristbands**; the fur and the torso front stay brown.

## Stock neighbours

Swordsman and Medieval Infantry.

## Rounds

### Build rounds 1-2
- The Dane axe raised in both hands, the head up by his right shoulder (the shipped low-across hold hid it). Civ
  12-26 %.

### Critic round 1 (batch 2): 65 / 57.5 = 61.25
Both: the off hand not on the haft in several directions; the axe head a pure white flag; barefoot; one: in direction
2 the axe foreshortens in front of the face (borderline critical). Fixes: the hands closer (grip2 0.34), the haft
leaning out to his right, iron-grey steel, boots.

### Critic round 2 (batch 2): 67 / 67 = 67
Landed: iron-grey head, boots, both fists in 0, 1, 7. Critical (one critic): in 4-6 the left hand is off the haft.
Also: a short haft for a Dane axe; the blue suit the odd one out. Fixes: the right fist lower so the left reaches
the haft (grip2 0.3), the haft 1.35, a broad belt.

### Critic round 3 (batch 2): 67 / 65.5 = 66.25
Landed: the longer haft, both fists in 0, 1, 7. Still: in direction 2 the haft runs up the front of the face, the
head hidden (borderline critical, both); one fist in 4-6; a yellow beard-and-braid mask; the blue suit the odd one
out. Extra round, aimed at direction 2 and the off hand: the haft diagonal across the front (the right fist at the
shoulder, the left low, grip2 0.55), the head up at his right.
The extra round as committed left the left fist past the haft's end (the battle_axe haft ran only 0.35 m below the
grip: grip2 0.55 had nothing to hold). Two build fixes: props.battle_axe 'below' (off by default: the haft's length
under the grip), and the axe steep at his right shoulder, leaning back, so the head clears the helmet in 2.

### Solo extra round (new continuity critic + fresh critic, review/melee/berserk_solo_r4.png): 60.5 / 56.5 = 58.5
Both: the steep hold reads as a pole or standard at 1x, the head edge-on in 2 and 6; the left hand unseen on the haft
in the back views (critical, both); the pose static, the sprite twice the roster's height. Point 1 (2) partly landed,
point 2 (the off hand) not in the back views.

## Final
Did not beat 67: **round 2 restored (67 / 67 = 67)**: the haft 1.1, the hands (-0.2, -0.22, -0.08), grip2 0.34, no
belt. Open: a diagonal two-handed carry with both fists visible from behind; a bigger blade broadside in every view;
folds in the civ wool.
