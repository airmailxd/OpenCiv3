# Medieval Japanese Leader: realism design (2026-11-23)

MDJ unit, remade in units3d: the daimyo.

## The real model
A daimyo in court dress: the tall lacquered **eboshi** court cap (its crown civ; riders 'eboshi'), a dark
wide-sleeved **kariginu** whose sleeves are in the civ colour, a pale under-collar, wide hakama to the ankle,
white tabi; the **gunbai**, the commander's lacquered war fan rimmed in gilt, raised in the right hand.

## What must read at 1x
1. The tall cap's upright silhouette;
2. the wide civ sleeves and the wide hakama: a broad, robed figure;
3. the round dark fan with its gilt rim raised before him.

## Pose
'royal': upright, the fan raised in the right fist; a light weight shift.

## Civ colour
The cap and the sleeves. Shipped 25% (22-28); stock Settler 12%, Worker 15%. Target 15-25%.

## Neighbours and size
Critic sheet: shipped, new, Samurai, Settler. Size counterpart: the Settler.
