# Impi: realism design (2026-11-23)

## The real-world model

A Zulu warrior of Shaka's and Cetshwayo's regiments, 1820s-1879 (Isandlwana; the drawings of the war of 1879):

- the **iklwa**: a short stabbing spear, a broad leaf blade of 30-45 cm on a shaft of about 75 cm;
- the **isihlangu**: a tall oval war shield of cowhide (about 1.2 m), the hide's colour marking the regiment,
  a stick laced up its back and held behind it;
- regimental dress: a headdress of feathers and fur, cow-tail ornaments at the arms and below the knees, a
  loin covering of hide and tails.

Civ3's original: crouched and bow-legged, the short broad spear low and forward, the tall cowhide shield
upright on the left arm, a civ feather headdress, civ arm and wrist bands, civ cow-tail tufts on the calves.

## The features that must read at 1x

1. The **tall black-and-white cowhide shield**, upright beside him, its face to the viewer.
2. The **iklwa held ready**: the right fist at the hip, the broad blade forward and up, bright (the stab
   comes from under the shield's rim).
3. The **civ feather headdress**.
4. Dark skin, the cow-tail tufts at the calves.

## The pose

A crouch with the torso upright (the knees bent, the feet wide), weight on the right leg; the shield held
forward on the left; the spear's blade forward out at his right side.

## Civ colour

Stock: the shipped Impi 16-33 % (mean 26). Aim 15-30 % in every direction. Regions: the **headdress**, the
**arm and wrist bands**, the **calf tufts**.

## Stock neighbours

Spearman and Warrior.

## Rounds

### Build rounds 1-2
- The iklwa ready at the hip, the isihlangu forward, then held by its grip. Civ 16-29 %.

### Critic round 1 (batch 2): 76 / 76.3 = 76.15
Both: the strongest of the batch; the spear reads as a long assegai, not the iklwa; one: the headdress dominates,
the kilt merges with the skin. Fixes: a short iklwa with a broad leaf (length 1.0, head 2.2), the headdress at 0.78,
a lighter hide kilt, darker steel.

### Critic round 2 (batch 2): 78.5 / 73 = 75.75
Landed: the short broad iklwa, the smaller headdress, the lighter kilt. Critical (one critic, near-critical the
other): in direction 6 the blade shows alone above the shoulder. Also: the isihlangu too short; glossy skin. Fixes:
the blade lower and more forward, a taller shield (0.6 x 1.32) held higher.

### Critic round 3 (batch 2): 78.5 / 79.5 = 79

## Final (round 3)
Scores: R1 76 / 76.3 = 76.15; R2 78.5 / 73 = 75.75; **R3 78.5 / 79.5 = 79, final** (cap; just under the 80 that
earns a solo round). No critical failure (direction 6's lone blade fixed for one critic, a small grey blade still
shows by the shoulder for the other). Civ 14-26 % (mean 20). Open points: matte skin with muscle shading (it reads
glossy); shaggy cow-tail fringes, not striped socks (the 'wraps' tufts); in 7 the shield edge-on with the blade
across it.

### Size check (after the final)
The final drew 1.10x the shipped opaque area (poles excluded): unit_scale 0.975, re-rendered with its animations (now x1.04). Not re-scored.
