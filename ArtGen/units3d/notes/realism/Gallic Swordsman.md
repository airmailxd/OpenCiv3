# Gallic Swordsman: realism design (2026-11-23)

## The real-world model

A Gallic warrior of the La Tène period, about 225-50 BC (Telamon, Alesia; the Dying Gaul, Polybius and Diodorus
on the gaesatae who fought naked, and the checked trousers, bracae, of the rest):

- a **long iron sword** (La Tène II-III, 75-90 cm blade, a rounded point, a bronze or iron hilt), slung on
  a chain belt at the right side;
- a **shield**: the tall oval or flat board of the Gauls with a spindle boss; Civ3 draws a small round one
  with a brown face, a silver boss and rim;
- **bracae**: checked wool trousers; bare chest; a torc; limed hair brushed up into spikes;
- boots or shoes.

Civ3's original: shirtless, ginger hair spiked up, check-patterned civ trousers, a civ band on the sword
forearm, a belt with a yellow pouch, a long sword low and angled down-forward, a small round shield.

## The features that must read at 1x

1. The **long sword held ready**: raised and out at his right side, the blade up and forward, bright with a
   dark hilt (the shipped one is a thin hairline low across the body).
2. **Spiked ginger hair** and a gold torc over a bare pale chest.
3. The **civ checked trousers**.
4. The **round shield** on the left forearm, upright and turned to the viewer.

## The pose

Weight on the right leg, the left foot forward; the shield forward on the left forearm; the right fist at
shoulder height out at his side, the long blade up and forward (a high guard, ready to cut down); the torso
upright, the head level.

## Civ colour

Stock: the shipped Gallic Swordsman 16-25 % (mean 20), Swordsman 7-15 (11), Legionary 22-34 (28). Aim 15-30 %
in every direction. Regions: the **trousers** (under the dark checks) and the **sword-arm band**; the shield
stays brown wood.

## Stock neighbours

Swordsman and Legionary.

## Rounds

### Build rounds 1-2
- The long sword raised at his right in the 'ready' stance; the shield first on the forearm, then held by its grip
  (the batch-1 fix), raised. Civ 16-24 %.

### Critic round 1 (batch 2): 70.8 / 63.5 = 67.15
Both: the torc reads as a dark collar; the civ forearm bracers make him look sleeved; the high guard reads only in
4-7 (low and forward in 0-2); stiff arms in 3 and 7. Fixes: a bright gold torc, bare arms with a civ armband on the
bicep, the sword more upright with the fist higher, the shield arm bent; steel darker.

### Critic round 2 (batch 2): 73.5 / 75 = 74.25
Landed: bare arms (civ ~16-18 %), the high guard in most directions. Overshot: the torc a broad gold yoke. Still: the
shield arm across the body in 3 and straight out in 7; brown hair, not limed. Fixes: a thin torc (0.014), the shield
arm in and bent, a paler ginger, a longer blade (1.0).

### Critic round 3 (batch 2): 75 / 75.5 = 75.25

## Final (round 3)
Scores: R1 70.8 / 63.5 = 67.15; R2 73.5 / 75 = 74.25; **R3 75 / 75.5 = 75.25, final** (cap). No critical failure.
Civ 14-18 % (mean 16). Open points: in direction 3 the forearms still cross to the shield and hilt, in 7 the shield
arm sticks out level (the shield's grip target is shared by all directions: a later pass could bring the shield
in toward the chest); the skin flat and the blade a hairline at 1x; the raised sword makes him taller than stock.
