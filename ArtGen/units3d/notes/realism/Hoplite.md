# Hoplite: realism design (2026-11-23)

## The real-world model

A Greek hoplite of the late Archaic and Classical age, about 500-450 BC (Marathon, Thermopylae, Plataea;
the vase painters and the Riace and Leonidas bronzes):

- the **aspis**: a round shield of about 90 cm, a wooden bowl faced with a thin bronze sheet, its offset rim
  standing out, carried on the forearm by the porpax (a bronze arm band at the centre) and the antilabe
  (a cord grip at the rim); a painted device on the face;
- the **Corinthian helmet** of bronze, closed over the face with eye openings and a nose guard, a tall
  horsehair crest on a stilt from front to back;
- a **bronze bell or muscle cuirass** (or a linothorax) over a short chiton, a skirt of **pteruges**;
- **bronze greaves** sprung round the shins;
- the **dory**: an ash spear of 2.2-2.7 m, a leaf head and a bronze butt-spike (the sauroter).

## The features that must read at 1x

1. The **big round bronze aspis**, upright, turned to the viewer, covering him from chin to knee on the left;
   its bronze face and black device, the civ rim.
2. The **Corinthian helmet with its tall crest** (civ).
3. The **spear held ready** over the shield: upright and a little forward, the head well above the crest,
   the shaft clear of the shield's rim and of the body's centre line.
4. Bronze cuirass and greaves, leather pteruges.

## The pose

The hoplite at the ready in the line: weight on the right leg, the left foot forward behind the shield; the
shield on the left forearm, held forward and upright, its face to the front-left; the right fist at chest
height on the spear, the spear upright and tipped forward a little; the torso upright, the head level.

## Civ colour

Stock: the shipped Hoplite 19-29 % (mean 23), Spearman 17-26 (21), Legionary 22-34 (28). Aim 15-30 % in every
direction. Regions: the **crest**, the **aspis rim**, and the **wristband / cuirass trim** (the original's).

## Stock neighbours

Spearman and Legionary.

## Rounds

### Build rounds 1-2
- The spear raised over the aspis; the aspis on the forearm with a porpax back, its inside bare wood.

### Critic round 1 (batch 1): 69 / 64 = 66.5
Both: the bronze is flat lemon plastic; the spear vertical and low beside the shield. Fixes: darker worn bronze with
a sheen (metallic 0.85, roughness 0.38), the spear raised higher and tipped forward, a larger crest.

### Critic round 2 (batch 1): 73 / 68 = 70.5
Landed: the spear over the rim, the crest. Still: butter-yellow bronze the hue of the skin, the shield face blown
out. Fixes: darker bronze (#7a5428), a darker bronze shield face, the aspis held by its grip.

### Critic round 3 (batch 1): 73 / 67 = 70
The darker bronze went the hue of the skin (he reads bare-chested); the shield's inner face blew out white in 2
and 7; the crest a hood in 3-4. Below round 2: restored.

## Final (round 2, restored)
Scores: R1 69 / 64 = 66.5; **R2 73 / 68 = 70.5, final**; R3 73 / 67 = 70. Round 2's bronze (#9a6e34) and the aspis
on the forearm restored; the re-render matches round 2's civ share to the pixel count (23 22 22 27 24 20 20 24).
No critical failure. Open points: bronze that is neither lemon nor skin-coloured (a cooler, greener bronze with
dark edges); the Corinthian helmet's eye slots and cheek-pieces; a tall narrow crest on a stilt; the shield's
inside consistent wood or leather.
