# Warrior: realism design (2026-11-23)

## The real-world model

A warrior of the European Copper Age, about 2800-2300 BC (the Corded Ware and Bell Beaker horizon; the
finds of Ötzi, of Talheim and Eulau): the man of a war band before metal armour.

- a **polished stone shaft-hole battle-axe**: a ground and drilled stone head (greenstone, diorite) on an ash
  haft of about 70-80 cm, the period's status weapon, carried in the graves of men;
- a **hide loincloth** on a leather belt, hide leggings or wraps bound below the knee;
- a **fur cape** over the shoulders against the cold;
- long hair and a beard; a woven band round the head.

Civ3's original is this figure: bare-chested, long hair, a civ-coloured loincloth and wrist wraps, striped
leg wraps and a stone-headed axe on a long haft.

## The features that must read at 1x

1. The **stone axe raised at the ready**: the pale stone head above his right shoulder, the haft in his fist
   in front of the shoulder, away from the body's centre line (the shipped club stuck out stiff from a
   straight arm).
2. The **bare, brawny torso under a fur cape**: a wide dark shoulder mass over skin.
3. **Long dark hair and beard** with the civ headband.
4. The **civ loincloth and wrist wraps** over striped leg wraps.

## The pose

Weight on the right (rear) leg, the left foot a half step forward, the knee soft; the pelvis rolled over the
weight leg, the shoulders counter-rolled; the torso upright. The right fist in front of the right shoulder
holding the haft near its end, the axe head up and a little back over the shoulder, the edge turned
forward-out (so it shows its profile in the diagonal views). The left fist forward at the belly, a guard.
The head level, turned a little toward the front.

## Civ colour

Stock foot units measure 9-34 % (tint pixels with any tint over the unit's opaque pixels, DEFAULT, per
direction): the shipped Warrior 12-17 (mean 14), Spearman 17-26 (21), Bowman 19-25 (21), Hoplite 19-29 (23).
Aim 15-30 % in every direction. Regions: the **loincloth**, the **wrist wraps**, and a **headband** (new: it
reads from behind, where the cape hides the wraps).

## Stock neighbours

Spearman and Archer (the ancient tier he stands beside).

## Rounds

### Build rounds 1-2
- The stone axe raised at the ready in the 'ready' stance (new, shared): it reads in every direction at 1x; the arm
  first stuck straight out sideways (elbow pole outward), then bent with the elbow down. Civ 15-21 %.

### Critic round 1 (batch 1): 65 / 65.5 = 65.25
Both: in direction 2 the axe head sits on the face; the head is a flat pale cleaver, not stone; the civ wrist wraps
read as gauntlets. Fixes: the fist further out at his right and the haft leaning out, a smaller dark greenstone
head, civ wrist bands instead of bracers.

### Critic round 2 (batch 1): 68 / 79 = 73.5
Landed: the wrist bands, the haft leaning out. Still: in direction 2 the head is lost against the shoulder and cape;
the head reads as pale metal. Fixes: the fist higher (above the shoulder) so the head clears the head line, a bigger
(scale 1.3) dark greenstone head.

### Critic round 3 (batch 1): 75 / 74 = 74.5
Landed: the axe clears the head in direction 2, a darker head. Still: a thin flat wedge for a stone head; the cape a
grey blob from behind; a dark face under the hair; the arm straight up in 6-7 reads as waving.

## Final (round 3)
Scores: R1 65 / 65.5 = 65.25; R2 68 / 79 = 73.5; **R3 75 / 74 = 74.5, final** (cap of three batch rounds). No
critical failure. Civ 12-17 % (mean 14). Open points: a thick stone lens with lashing for the head; a darker ragged
pelt with a tie; the face lit under the hair; the axe arm bent with the haft back over the shoulder in 6-7.
