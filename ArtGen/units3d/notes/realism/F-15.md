# F-15 (realism pass): design

An F-15C Eagle: the big shoulder-mounted wing with cropped tips, the boxy
intakes beside the cockpit, twin upright fins, the bubble canopy,
missiles under the wings. Two-tone Mod Eagle grey (hard-edged darker
patches), lighter underside. Neighbours: the stock Tank and Helicopter.

## Baseline (as shipped)
Pale grey, thin civ stripes along the wings' leading edges (stair-stepped
on the coarse wing faces), civ fin tips and a spine stripe. Tint 4.4-6.8 %
(mean 5.0).

## What must read at 1x
1. The boxy intakes and the large wing, the twin upright fins.
2. Civ colour in three places: a squadron band on the fins' upper halves,
   the spine stripe and the tailplane tips. Aim 5-8 %.
3. Mid-grey values, darker than the shipped.

## Build rounds (own, before batch critic round 1)
- R1: darker two-tone grey with big hard patches; the wing stripes
  (stair-stepped) replaced by the fin band and the spine stripe.
- R2: darker (#5e666e / #4a525a); tailplane tips added (front and rear
  views carried 2 % civ only); the tailplanes resampled (step 0.2: one
  face spanned the whole tailplane, so all of it went civ, 14 %).
- R3: trimmed: fin band from z 2.7, tailplane tips from 3.6 m out.
Batch 1 sheet: tint 6.2 / 4.0 / 6.2 / 8.1 / 7.7 / 4.9 / 7.7 / 8.1 % (mean
6.6; shipped 5.0). Footprint 99.9 %. specks.py clean.
