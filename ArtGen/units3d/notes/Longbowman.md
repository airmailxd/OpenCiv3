# Longbowman
- Pose: upright stance; left hand holds a tall longbow vertically in front of the body with an arrow nocked (string pale/white, not drawn); right hand near the string at chest height.
- Gear: rounded pale-gold metal cap/helmet with a curling feather plume at the back; knee-length sleeveless tunic belted at the waist over gray mail sleeves; gray trousers; black boots; large tan leather quiver/arrow bag slung diagonally at the right hip and back; shoulder strap across the chest.
- Colors: blue tunic, pale gold helmet (#d0c8a8), mail gray (#9a9488), gray trousers (#8e887c), black boots, tan quiver, brown bow (#6a4a28).
- Tinted (civ color): the tunic (front and back, to mid-thigh) and the curved plume on the helmet.
- Versus old remake: catalog uses a 'hood' but the original wears a rounded metal cap; the quiver is a big tan bag hanging low at the hip, not a small back quiver; bow is held upright with arrow nocked.
