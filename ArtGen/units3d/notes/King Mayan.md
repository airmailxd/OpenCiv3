# King Mayan
- Pose: upright; arms folded across the chest, with the right hand poking out to the side holding a small gold object.
- Gear: tall headdress: crest on top, small red and green feathers at front, and a long cascade of white feathers down the back to the waist; gold face ornaments; gold collar and arm bands; spotted jaguar-skin kilt/tunic; ankle-length cape behind with gold dotted hem; bare legs, sandals.
- Colors: gold/jaguar (#c8a040 with dark spots #4a3018), white feathers (#e8e4da), red/green accents, brown skin.
- Tinted (civ color): the headdress crest and the full back cape (a few tinted beads on chest).
- Versus old remake: crest is tinted, not green with tinted bits; cape reaches the ankles, not hip length; otherwise matches.
