# Detail Audit: Blender remakes vs Civ3 originals (PC sheets)

Rule: a remake needs at least as much detail as the original. Every item under **Missing** blocks the unit.
Sources: `_previews/units3d/vehicles4_sheet.png`, `riders_a.png`, `riders_b.png`, `chariots_sheet.png` (zoomed crops of the original and Blender rows, all 8 directions).
Crews on the Catapult and Cannon are left out on purpose and are not counted.

**Update 2026-10-16:** the Catapult, Cannon, Galley and Caravel items below were added (commits af9548e1, dc479548, 60cffdf8, 06db7417). All four PASS the 6-axis review with detail 7-8.

---

## 1. Catapult
**Missing / simplified**
- [ ] Iron winch/ratchet gear: the original has a light-grey toothed cog wheel on the side of the base frame (very visible in the SW, S and E views). Blender has no gear.
- [ ] Iron crank handles: grey levers stick out sideways from the winch axle on both sides (clearly visible in the N view). Missing.
- [ ] Iron throwing cup: the original arm ends in a large round iron bucket with a pale rim and a blue inner lining. Blender has a small dark lump.
- [ ] Twisted torsion/rope bundle at the arm pivot (dark coiled skein between the side frames). Missing.
- [ ] Spare stone ammunition: a grey block or stone lies on or beside the frame. Missing.
- [ ] Wheel detail: the original wheels are dark brown with iron hub caps and iron tires. Blender wheels are plain dark discs with no hub or tire contrast.
- [ ] Wood tone and edges: the original frame is pale golden pine with highlighted beam edges. Blender is uniformly dark brown, so the beam joints get lost (keep the color, but the beams need edge bevels or highlight trim).
- [ ] Blue panels: the original fills the lower side rectangles of the base with blue boards. Blender has only thin blue rails.

**Extras to exceed the original:** iron corner brackets with rivets on frame joints; rope lashing wrapped around the crossbar where the arm strikes; wedge pins through the axle ends.

**Detail vs original: 4/10**

## 2. Cannon
**Missing / simplified**
- [ ] Barrel banding: the original barrel is bright polished steel or pewter with a raised muzzle swell, a reinforcing ring and a breech knob (cascabel). Blender's barrel is dark, smooth and has no rings.
- [ ] Wheel rims: the original has light silver iron tires around blue wheels with brown felloes, so there are three tones. Blender has a blue rim and blue spokes only. Add the iron tire and the wooden felloe band.
- [ ] Wheel hubs: the original hub shows a large iron hub cap and an axle pin. Blender's hub is minimal.
- [ ] Trail: the original trail carries blue paint with iron straps and the trail plate. Blender's trail is plain brown wood with no strapping.
- [ ] Elevation screw or quoin under the breech. Missing.

**Extras to exceed the original:** trunnion cap-squares with bolts; lifting handles (dolphins) on the barrel; a rammer/sponge rod and bucket hung on the trail.

**Detail vs original: 5/10**

## 3. Galley
**Missing / simplified**
- [ ] Sail edge bands: the original has wide solid-blue vertical bands on both sail edges (about 15% of the width each). Blender has only thin blue lines.
- [ ] Sail emblem: the original "double crescent" has dotted fill and thick strokes. Blender's strokes are thin outlines.
- [ ] Gold masthead finial: the original has a large bright gold knob or lantern at the top of the mast. Blender's is tiny and dull.
- [ ] Gold hull trim: the original has a pale gold strake (wale band) along the hull below the rail, plus gold highlights on the stem and stern posts. Blender's hull is all one brown with no wale.
- [ ] Stern canopy: the original has a blue-and-white striped awning at the stern. Blender has a flat plain blue panel.
- [ ] Bow ornament: the original has a blue crescent/eye at the bow (seen head-on) and a curled gold sternpost (aplustre). Blender's bow is only partly marked and its sternpost is brown.
- [ ] Rowers' bench or oar-port detail: the original shows dark oar ports along the hull. Blender's oars go straight into a plain hull.

**Extras to exceed the original:** bronze ram at the waterline; painted eye on both sides of the bow; rope lashing around the hull (hypozomata) and a steering oar with a tiller.

**Detail vs original: 5/10**

## Common gaps (summary)
1. **Carried and side items dropped:** the Conquistador's war dog, the Keshik's twin lances, the Cossack's rifle and gazyri, sabres and scabbards (Hussar, Cavalry, Cossack, Ansar), shields shrunk to small white discs, and chariot-mounted quivers.
2. **Horse tack reduced to one rectangular saddle cloth:** breast straps, cruppers, tassels, chanfrons and crinets, neck-covering caparisons (Sipahi, Knight) and emblems on the cloth are missing.
3. **Gold/brass trim:** gold borders, finials, frogging, collars, wales and yoke ornaments are consistently absent.
4. **Pattern and emblem fidelity:** sail bands, crosses, panel emblems and dotted fills are simplified.
5. **Ship rigging and hardware:** ratlines, fighting tops, hull wales, and wheel tires and hubs on siege engines.
6. **Coat and color variety lost:** dun/dapple/mixed teams have been replaced with flat single colors.
